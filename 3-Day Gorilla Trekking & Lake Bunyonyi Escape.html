Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN KIGALI & TRANSFER TO BWINDI IMPENETRABLE NATIONAL PARK

Upon arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional guide from Mahogany Travel Expeditions.

Your adventure begins with a scenic drive through Rwanda's famous rolling hills before crossing into Uganda via the Cyanika Border or Katuna Border.

Enjoy spectacular countryside views, lush tea plantations, local villages, and dramatic mountain scenery as you journey toward the magnificent Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Arrive at your comfortable forest lodge in the afternoon, where you can relax while soaking in the tranquil surroundings of the ancient rainforest.

In the evening, your guide will brief you on everything you need to know about tomorrow's gorilla trekking experience, including trekking guidelines, safety procedures, and what to expect during your unforgettable encounter.

Accommodation:

Lodge near Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 2: GORILLA TREKKING & TRANSFER TO LAKE BUNYONYI

Wake up early to the sounds of the forest and enjoy a hearty breakfast before heading to the park headquarters.

After registration and a briefing from Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers, venture into the dense rainforest accompanied by experienced trackers in search of one of Bwindi's mountain gorilla families.

The trek can take anywhere from 1 to 6 hours, depending on the gorillas' movements, but every step through this ancient rainforest is part of the adventure.

Once you find the gorillas, you'll spend an awe-inspiring one full hour observing their fascinating behaviors—watching playful infants, protective silverbacks, and nurturing mothers in their natural environment.

After this once-in-a-lifetime experience, return to the park headquarters where you'll receive your official Gorilla Trekking Certificate.

Following lunch, continue your journey to the breathtaking Lake Bunyonyi, one of Africa's deepest and most picturesque lakes.

Check in to your lakeside lodge and spend the evening enjoying spectacular sunset views over the calm waters, surrounded by lush green hills and scattered islands.

Accommodation:

Lakeside Lodge at Lake Bunyonyi

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 3: LAKE BUNYONYI BOAT CRUISE & RETURN TO KIGALI

Wake up to the peaceful beauty of Lake Bunyonyi and enjoy breakfast overlooking its calm waters.

After breakfast, embark on a relaxing guided boat cruise, exploring some of the lake's famous islands, including the historic Punishment Island, while learning about the fascinating stories and culture of the local communities.

Take in the breathtaking scenery, spot colorful birdlife, and capture unforgettable photographs of one of East Africa's most beautiful landscapes.

Return to the lodge for lunch before beginning your scenic drive back to Kigali.

Upon arrival in Kigali, your guide will transfer you to Kigali International Airport or your preferred hotel, bringing your unforgettable gorilla and lakeside adventure to a memorable close.

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch

Essential Trekking Tips

What to Pack

Hiking Boots: Sturdy, waterproof boots with good ankle support. Layered Clothing: Moisture-wicking base layer, long-sleeved shirt, and waterproof jacket. Gardening Gloves: For gripping vegetation during steep sections. Daypack: For water, snacks, camera, and rain gear. Camera: With extra batteries and memory cards.

Physical Preparation

Fitness Level: Moderate fitness recommended - trekking can be challenging. Altitude: Bwindi is at 2,000-2,500m - stay hydrated. Pacing: Trek at your own pace - it's not a race!

Health & Safety

Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate required for entry to Rwanda and Uganda. Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation coverage. Porter Hire: Consider hiring a porter - supports locals and makes trek easier.

Best Time to Visit

Dry Seasons (Best): June-September & December-February - easier trekking conditions. Wet Seasons: March-May & October-November - lush scenery, fewer tourists. Year-Round: Gorilla trekking operates year-round.
