5-DAY LUXURY GORILLA AND WILDLIFE SAFARI

Premium Uganda Safari Experience

Experience the ultimate luxury safari combining Uganda's two most iconic wildlife experiences: mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and exceptional game viewing in Queen Elizabeth National Park. This 5-day luxury safari offers unparalleled comfort, exclusive access, and personalized service throughout your journey, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Luxury Accommodation

Stay in Uganda's finest lodges with premium amenities and stunning locations

Private Vehicle

Exclusive use of luxury 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof

Dedicated Guide

Personal safari guide with extensive wildlife expertise

Gourmet Dining

Fine dining experiences with premium wines and local delicacies

Luxury Safari Highlights

  • Exclusive Gorilla Trekking: Private gorilla family allocation for more intimate experience
  • Luxury Lodge Stays: Uganda's top-rated luxury accommodations with spectacular views
  • Private Game Drives: Exclusive vehicle for personalized wildlife viewing
  • Kazinga Channel Cruise: Luxury boat safari with premium viewing positions
  • Wellness Amenities: Spa treatments, swimming pools, and relaxation facilities
  • Gourmet Experiences: Fine dining with international and local cuisine
  • Personalized Service: Dedicated staff including guide, driver, and lodge concierge

Detailed Luxury Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL & LUXURY TRANSFER TO QUEEN ELIZABETH

Luxury Arrival: VIP meet and greet at Entebbe International Airport with champagne welcome. Transfer to private lounge for refreshments while your luggage is handled.

Exclusive Charter Flight (Optional): For the ultimate luxury experience, upgrade to a private charter flight to Queen Elizabeth National Park (45 minutes vs 7-hour drive).

Luxury Road Transfer: Travel in premium air-conditioned 4x4 vehicle with refreshments, Wi-Fi, and panoramic views. Stop at the Equator for luxury photo opportunity.

Arrival at Luxury Lodge: Arrive at your exclusive lodge overlooking the park. Welcome ceremony with traditional music and cooling towels.

Private Sundowner: Evening sundowner drinks at a private viewpoint overlooking the savannah, with canapés served by your personal butler.

Gourmet Welcome Dinner: Multi-course dinner featuring fusion of international and Ugandan cuisine, paired with premium wines.

Accommodation: Overnight at luxury lodge suite with private veranda, outdoor shower, and butler service.

DAY 2: PRIVATE GAME DRIVES & LUXURY BOAT SAFARI

Sunrise Game Drive: Private early morning game drive in exclusive sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park. Your expert guide will track lions, elephants, buffalo, and more.

Bush Breakfast: Set up in a scenic location, enjoy a gourmet bush breakfast with freshly prepared dishes and barista coffee.

Leisure Time: Return to lodge for relaxation. Enjoy spa treatments, swimming pool, or private nature walk with lodge naturalist.

Luxury Lunch: Alfresco lunch featuring local ingredients and international favorites.

Kazinga Channel Luxury Cruise: Private boat safari on the Kazinga Channel. Premium front-row seating for optimal hippo, crocodile, and bird photography.

Evening Game Drive: Late afternoon private game drive focusing on nocturnal wildlife and predator activity.

Private Bush Dinner: Exclusive bush dinner under the stars with traditional entertainment and fine dining.

DAY 3: TRANSFER TO BWINDI & LUXURY PREPARATION

Scenic Transfer: Leisurely drive through the beautiful landscapes of southwestern Uganda. Stop at panoramic viewpoints for photography.

Luxury Picnic Lunch: Gourmet picnic set up at a scenic location with views of the Virunga volcanoes.

Arrival at Gorilla Lodge: Check into your luxury gorilla lodge with spectacular forest views. Personal butler assigned for duration of stay.

Gorilla Trekking Preparation: Private briefing with senior guide covering gorilla behavior, photography tips, and luxury amenities available during trek.

Luxury Gear Provision: High-end trekking equipment provided including premium hiking boots, waterproof gear, and professional camera equipment.

Spa Preparation: Pre-trek massage and relaxation treatment to prepare for tomorrow's adventure.

Gourmet Dinner: Fine dining experience with focus on healthy, energy-boosting cuisine for next day's trek.

DAY 4: EXCLUSIVE GORILLA TREKKING EXPERIENCE

Luxury Morning: Early wake-up with premium coffee/tea service in your room. Nutritious gourmet breakfast.

Private Park Entry: Exclusive early access to park headquarters with private registration area.

Personalized Trek Planning: Consultation with head ranger to select gorilla family based on your preferences and fitness level.

Luxury Trek Support: Full support team including senior guide, assistant guide, armed ranger, and personal porter for each guest.

Gorilla Encounter: Spend one magical hour with mountain gorillas. Private viewing area for your group only.

Luxury Forest Lunch: Gourmet lunch set up in the forest with proper table setting and chilled beverages.

Return & Celebration: Return to lodge for celebration drinks and certificate presentation.

Wellness Recovery: Post-trek spa treatments including massage, hydrotherapy, and relaxation rituals.

Celebration Dinner: Special celebration dinner with champagne toast and personalized menu.

DAY 5: LEISURE & LUXURY DEPARTURE

Leisurely Morning: Late wake-up call with in-room breakfast option. Time for final relaxation at lodge amenities.

Optional Activities: Choose from luxury experiences:

  • Helicopter scenic flight over Bwindi and volcanoes
  • Private guided forest walk focusing on birds and primates
  • Cultural visit to local community with exclusive access
  • Additional spa and wellness treatments

Gourmet Farewell Lunch: Final luxury lunch at the lodge with personalized menu.

Luxury Transfer: Return journey in premium comfort with refreshments and entertainment options.

VIP Airport Service: For international departures, VIP airport assistance with fast-track services through security and immigration.

Optional Extension: Extend your luxury experience with additional nights, private island retreat on Lake Victoria, or continuation to Rwanda for golden monkey trekking.

Luxury Accommodation Options

This safari features Uganda's most exclusive properties. Sample luxury lodges include:

Queen Elizabeth National Park:

  • Mweya Safari Lodge: Premier location on peninsula with spectacular views, swimming pool, and fine dining
  • Kyambura Gorge Lodge: Exclusive lodge with private plunge pools, spa, and chimpanzee tracking access
  • Ishasha Wilderness Camp: Luxury tented camp in remote sector famous for tree-climbing lions

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest:

  • Clouds Mountain Gorilla Lodge: Ultra-luxury lodge with fireplace suites and private butlers
  • Bwindi Lodge: Luxury accommodation with spa, viewing deck, and gourmet restaurant
  • Sanctuary Gorilla Forest Camp: Exclusive tented camp with private verandas and forest views

All properties feature: King-size beds, premium linens, en-suite bathrooms with luxury amenities, private viewing decks, Wi-Fi, swimming pools, spa facilities, and exceptional dining.

    • Sun protection and high-quality binoculars
    • Camera equipment with telephoto lenses
  • Health & Wellness:
    • Private doctor consultation available if needed

    • Special dietary requirements accommodated with advance notice
    • Spa and wellness treatments can be pre-booked
  • Exclusive Services:
    • Private butler service at all accommodations
    • Personal safari guide throughout journey
    • Luxury vehicle with refreshments and Wi-Fi
