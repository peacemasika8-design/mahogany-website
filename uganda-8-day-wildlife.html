Experience the Pearl of Africa

This 8-day luxury Uganda wildlife and primate safari is an amazing and popular tour package offered by Mahogany Travel Expeditions. This safari is particularly designed to enable travelers to discover some of the hidden treasures of Uganda, known as the "Pearl of Africa." Experience diverse ecosystems, incredible wildlife, and unforgettable primate encounters across Uganda's most iconic national parks.

Overall Difficulty:
Moderate (some strenuous trekking involved)

Safari Highlights:

  • Mountain Gorilla Trekking: One-hour encounter with mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park
  • Chimpanzee Tracking: Search for chimpanzees in Kyambura Gorge, Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • Big 4 Wildlife: Game drives to spot lions, leopards, elephants, and buffalos
  • Scenic Boat Cruises: On the Nile River at Murchison Falls and Kazinga Channel
  • Rhino Tracking: At Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary - home to Uganda's only wild rhinos
  • Cultural Experiences: Batwa community tour and Ankole culture visit
  • Iconic Landscapes: Murchison Falls, Lake Bunyonyi, Rwenzori Mountains, and the Equator

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: TRANSFER TO MURCHISON FALLS NATIONAL PARK VIA ZIWA RHINO SANCTUARY

Arrival & Welcome: Meet your professional tour guide who will brief you about the 8-day luxury Uganda wildlife and primate safari.

Rhino Tracking: Start your safari to Murchison Falls National Park through Gulu Highway with a stopover at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for an unforgettable rhino trekking experience.

Journey to Murchison: After rhino tracking, drive to Masindi town for lunch, then proceed to Murchison Falls National Park.

Accommodation: Check in at your lodge for overnight stay.

Meal Plan: Breakfast and Dinner (Half Board)

DAY 2: MORNING GAME DRIVE AND AFTERNOON BOAT CRUISE ON THE NILE RIVER

Early Morning Game Drive: After breakfast (or packed breakfast), set off at 6:30 AM for a game drive in Murchison Falls National Park. The park is home to Africa's Big 4 animals (lions, leopards, elephants, and buffalos), various antelopes, birds, and beautiful savannah vegetation.

Afternoon Boat Cruise: Return to the lodge for lunch, then set off for an afternoon boat cruise on the Nile River (the world's longest river). Enjoy the serene beauty, spot hippopotamus, Nile crocodiles, and numerous water birds.

Hike to Top of Falls: Hike to the top of Murchison Falls for a thrilling adventure and breathtaking views.

Accommodation: Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner (Full Board)

Accommodation Options for Day 2:

Budget: Jipiri and Labongo Safari Lodge, Murchison River Lodge, Nile Treatz, Global Village, Ikorom Safari Lodge

Mid-range: Sambya River Lodge, Apoka Safari Lodge, Pakuba Safari Lodge

Luxury: Nile Safari Lodge, Entiko Safari Lodge, Para Safari Lodge

DAY 3: TRANSFER TO QUEEN ELIZABETH NATIONAL PARK

Scenic Drive: After breakfast, transfer to the famous Queen Elizabeth National Park, driving through the snow-capped Rwenzori Mountains and the western arm of Africa's Great Rift Valley.

Lunch Stop: Stop in Fort Portal city for lunch.

Equator Experience: Continue your safari with a stopover at the new Equator monument to create memorable photos.

Accommodation: Arrive at Queen Elizabeth National Park and check into your lodge for overnight.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner (Half Board)

DAY 4: CHIMPANZEE TRACKING AND AFTERNOON BOAT CRUISE

Chimpanzee Tracking: After an early breakfast, visit Kyambura Gorge for chimpanzee tracking. Start with a briefing, then begin your search for the chimpanzees. Once you meet them, you'll spend one hour observing their behavior.

Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise: In the afternoon, enjoy a boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel, which connects Lake Edward to Lake George. Spot schools of hippos, birds, crocodiles, and other wildlife.

Sunset Views: In the evening, enjoy beautiful sunset views of the Rwenzori Mountains.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Accommodation Options for Day 4:

Budget: Elephant Home Camp, Simba Safari Lodge, Engiri Game Lodge, Kazinga Channel Eco Villa, Pumba Safari Cottages

Mid-range: Buffalo Safari Lodge, Twin Lake Safari Lodge, Katara Lodge, Acacia Wilderness Mweya, Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge

Luxury: The River Station, Volcano Safari Lodge, Lake George View Golf Resort, Elephant Plains, Mweya Safari Lodge

DAY 5: MORNING GAME DRIVE AND TRANSFER TO BWINDI IMPENETRABLE NATIONAL PARK

Ishasha Sector Game Drive: After breakfast, take an extensive game drive in the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park. This sector is famous for its rare tree-climbing lions. Also spot elephants, buffalos, leopards, warthogs, kobs, and many other animals.

Transfer to Bwindi: Have lunch along the way as you drive to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Accommodation: Upon arrival, check into your lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

DAY 6: MOUNTAIN GORILLA TREKKING AND BATWA COMMUNITY TOUR

Gorilla Trekking Briefing: After breakfast, drive to the park headquarters for a briefing about gorilla trekking etiquette and safety.

Gorilla Trekking: Led by an experienced ranger, search for the mountain gorillas. Once you find a gorilla family, you'll spend one hour observing them. Take photos while following all rules and regulations.

Batwa Cultural Experience: Return to the lodge for lunch, then prepare for an evening walk to a neighboring Batwa village for a social and cultural exchange. Share African traditional stories, enjoy local brew, and dances with the community people.

Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi: After the cultural experience, transfer to Lake Bunyonyi.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner (Full Board)

Accommodation Options for Day 6:

Budget: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge, Bwind Guest House, CTPH Gorilla Conservation

Mid-range: Buhoma Lodge Bwindi, Gorilla Hills Lodge, Rafiki Safari Lodge Bwindi

Luxury: Nkuringo Safari Lodge, Buhoma Gorilla Camp, Silverback, Orugano Bwind Lodge, Chameleon Hill Forest Lodge, Ihamba Residence

DAY 7: LAKE BUNYONYI BOAT CRUISE AND GAME DRIVE IN LAKE MBURO NATIONAL PARK

Lake Bunyonyi Boat Cruise: After breakfast, set off for a boat cruise at Lake Bunyonyi, cruising through the numerous "punishment islands." Interact with locals and see various bird species.

Transfer to Lake Mburo: Embark on the drive to Lake Mburo National Park with lunch along the way.

Evening Game Drive: Upon arrival and check-in, enjoy an evening game drive in Lake Mburo National Park, spotting giraffes, zebras, kobs, buffalos, and various birds.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

Accommodation Options for Day 7:

Budget: Mburo Eagles Nest, Leopard Rest Camp, Hyena Hill Lodge

Mid-range: Mburo Safari Lodge, Arcadia Lodge, Rwakobo Rock

Luxury: Kigambira Safari Lodge, Kigarama Wilderness Lodge, Mihingo Lodge

DAY 8: GUIDED NATURE WALK AND TRANSFER BACK TO KAMPALA

Morning Nature Walk: After breakfast, take a guided walk around Lake Mburo National Park.

Ankole Culture Visit: Visit the Ankole culture communities and see the famous long-horned Ankole cattle.

Return to Kampala: Drive back to Kampala with lunch en route.

Departure: Your driver will drop you at your hotel or Entebbe International Airport for your outbound flight, marking the end of your 8-day luxury Uganda wildlife and primate safari.

Meal Plan: Breakfast and Lunch
