4-DAY TARANGIRE & LAKE MANYARA SAFARI

Tanzania Safari Elephants Baobab Trees Lake Manyara Birdwatching

Elephants Among Ancient Baobabs & Tree-Climbing Lions

Embark on a 4-day safari combining two of Tanzania's most diverse and beautiful parks - Tarangire National Park, famous for its massive elephant herds and ancient baobab trees, and Lake Manyara National Park, home to the famous tree-climbing lions and spectacular birdlife. This safari offers an incredible diversity of landscapes and wildlife in a compact itinerary, perfect for those wanting to experience the best of Tanzania's Northern Circuit without the crowds of the Serengeti.

Safari Difficulty:
Easy (Game drives in comfortable 4x4 vehicles, minimal walking)
3,000+
Elephants in Tarangire during dry season (one of Africa's largest populations)

Safari Highlights:

  • Elephant Spectacle: Witness one of Africa's largest elephant populations in Tarangire
  • Ancient Baobabs: Photograph wildlife against the backdrop of thousand-year-old baobab trees
  • Tree-Climbing Lions: Search for Lake Manyara's famous lions that lounge in acacia trees
  • Birdwatcher's Paradise: Over 550 bird species across both parks, including thousands of flamingos
  • Diverse Landscapes: From Tarangire's riverine forests to Lake Manyara's groundwater forest
  • Cultural Interaction: Optional visits to Maasai villages (at extra cost)
  • Photography: Excellent opportunities for wildlife and landscape photography
  • Less Crowded: Experience Tanzania's wildlife without the crowds of more famous parks

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: ARUSHA/MOSHI TO TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK

Morning Pickup: Collection from your hotel in Arusha or Moshi, or from Kilimanjaro Airport.

Scenic Drive: Journey southwest through the Maasai Steppe with views of Mount Meru.

Enter Tarangire: Arrive at Tarangire National Park gate around mid-morning.

First Impressions: Immediate sightings of elephants, baobabs, and diverse wildlife.

Morning Game Drive: Explore the park's northern sector with its large mammal concentrations.

Lunch at Picnic Site: Enjoy a picnic lunch at one of Tarangire's scenic viewpoints.

Afternoon Game Drive: Continue wildlife viewing, focusing on the Tarangire River area.

Elephant Behavior: Watch elephants drinking, bathing, and socializing at water sources.

Sunset Game Drive: Late afternoon wildlife viewing as animals become more active.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your lodge/camp just outside the park.

Evening Relaxation: Time to freshen up and enjoy lodge facilities.

Welcome Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with briefing about the next day's activities.

Night Sounds: Listen to the sounds of the African night from your accommodation.

DAY 2: FULL DAY TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK

Early Breakfast: Hearty breakfast before morning game drive.

Morning Game Drive: Enter the park early for the best wildlife viewing conditions.

Predator Search: Look for lions, leopards, and cheetahs during their active morning hours.

Baobab Photography: Photograph wildlife against the iconic baobab trees.

Silale Swamp: Visit this important wetland area teeming with birdlife and animals.

Lunch at Lodge: Return to lodge for lunch and midday rest (or picnic lunch in park).

Afternoon Options: Choose between:

  • Additional Game Drive: Explore different areas of Tarangire
  • Lodge Relaxation: Rest at lodge, swim, or enjoy the views
  • Cultural Visit: Optional visit to nearby Maasai village (extra cost)
  • Guided Nature Walk: Around lodge area with armed guide (if available)

Late Afternoon Game Drive: Return to park for evening wildlife viewing.

Sundowner: Enjoy drinks at a scenic spot as the sun sets over Tarangire.

Return to Lodge: Transfer back to your accommodation.

Evening Presentation: Some lodges offer talks about Tarangire's ecology.

Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with opportunity to share sightings.

DAY 3: TARANGIRE TO LAKE MANYARA NATIONAL PARK

Breakfast & Check-out: Final breakfast at Tarangire lodge before departure.

Morning Game Drive en route: Game drive through Tarangire as you exit the park.

Scenic Transfer: Drive to Lake Manyara National Park (approximately 2 hours).

Enter Lake Manyara: Arrive at Lake Manyara National Park gate.

Park Introduction: Briefing about Lake Manyara's unique features and wildlife.

Groundwater Forest: Drive through the dense groundwater forest at park entrance.

Tree-Climbing Lions: Begin search for Lake Manyara's famous lions in acacia trees.

Lunch at Picnic Site: Enjoy lunch with views of the Rift Valley escarpment.

Afternoon Game Drive: Explore different habitats:

  • Lake Shore: Flamingos, pelicans, and other waterbirds
  • Acacia Woodland: Giraffe, elephant, and buffalo
  • Alkaline Lakes: Spectacular bird concentrations
  • Hot Springs: Visit the Maji Moto hot springs

Birdwatching: Excellent opportunities for bird photography.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your Lake Manyara accommodation.

Evening Relaxation: Time to enjoy lodge facilities with views of the Rift Valley.

Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with views of Lake Manyara.

DAY 4: LAKE MANYARA & RETURN TO ARUSHA/MOSHI

Optional Morning Activity: Choose between:

  • Morning Game Drive: Final chance to see tree-climbing lions
  • Birdwatching Walk: Guided walk around lodge area (if available)
  • Cultural Interaction: Visit to local community project (optional)

Breakfast: Final breakfast at Lake Manyara lodge.

Check-out: Depart from your accommodation.

Morning Game Drive: Final game drive in Lake Manyara (if not done earlier).

Exit Park: Depart Lake Manyara National Park.

Return Journey: Scenic drive back to Arusha or Moshi.

Lunch en route: Stop for lunch at a restaurant with Rift Valley views.

Arusha/Moshi Arrival: Arrive by mid-afternoon.

Final Options:

  • Transfer to Kilimanjaro Airport for departure
  • Drop-off at your Arusha/Moshi hotel if extending your stay
  • Visit to Arusha Cultural Heritage Centre if time permits

Farewell: Bid farewell to your guide and driver.

Souvenir Shopping: Optional stop at craft markets (if time allows).

Extension Suggestions: Combine with Ngorongoro Crater, Serengeti National Park, or extend with relaxation at Lake Manyara's treehouse lodges.

Why This Combination Works Perfectly

These two parks complement each other perfectly. Tarangire offers vast landscapes and massive elephant herds, while Lake Manyara provides intimate wildlife encounters in a compact, diverse setting. The proximity (just 2 hours apart) makes this combination ideal for a shorter safari without wasting time on long transfers. You experience two completely different ecosystems in one seamless itinerary.

Safari Rules & Regulations:

  • Park Hours: Generally 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM (varies slightly by season)
  • Stay in Vehicle: No getting out of vehicle except at designated areas
  • No Off-Road Driving: Strictly stay on designated tracks
  • Speed Limits: Observe park speed limits (typically 40-50 km/h)
  • No Feeding Animals: Strictly prohibited in all Tanzanian parks
  • Litter: All waste must be carried out - practice "leave no trace"
  • Noise: Keep voices low to avoid disturbing wildlife
  • Photography: No flash photography of animals at close range
  • Guide Instructions: Always follow your guide's directions for safety
Climate & Altitude

Tarangire sits at 1,100 meters (3,600 feet) and Lake Manyara at 960 meters (3,150 feet). Both parks have a pleasant climate year-round. Daytime temperatures typically range from 25-30°C (77-86°F), while nights can cool to 15-20°C (59-68°F). The dry season (June-October) is cooler and less humid, while the wet season (November-May) brings warmer temperatures and occasional afternoon showers.

Essential Tips for Your Safari

Photography Tips

Golden Hours: Best photography is early morning and late afternoon. Baobab Silhouettes: Sunset behind baobabs creates stunning silhouettes. Tree-Climbing Lions: Use a telephoto lens for lions in trees. Bird Photography: Faster shutter speeds (1/1000+) for birds in flight.

Wildlife Viewing Strategy

Early Starts: Animals are most active in early morning. Water Sources: Concentrate around rivers and waterholes in Tarangire. Acacia Trees: Scan acacia trees carefully for lions in Lake Manyara. Patience: Spend time at good sightings rather than rushing between them.

Birdwatching in Both Parks

Tarangire River: Excellent for raptors and woodland birds. Lake Manyara Shore: Best for waterbirds and flamingos. Groundwater Forest: Unique forest species in Lake Manyara. Seasonal Variations: Migratory birds present November-April.

Health & Safety

Malaria Prophylaxis: Recommended for Tanzania (consult your doctor). Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation coverage. Sun Protection: High altitude means stronger UV rays. Hydration: Drink plenty of water throughout the day.

Best Time for This Safari:

  • Dry Season (June-October): Best for Tarangire elephants concentrated around water
  • Green Season (November-May): Beautiful landscapes, migratory birds, fewer tourists
  • Birding Peak: November-April for migratory birds in both parks
  • Flamingo Season: November-May for maximum flamingo numbers at Lake Manyara
  • Photography: June-July for clear skies, January-February for green landscapes
  • Avoiding Crowds: April-May & November (except holidays) for fewer vehicles
Conservation & Community Impact

Your safari directly supports conservation efforts in both parks. Park fees fund anti-poaching patrols, habitat restoration, and wildlife research. Many lodges employ locally, purchase from local suppliers, and support community projects. Optional cultural visits provide direct income to Maasai communities. By choosing responsible operators, your visit contributes to sustainable tourism in Tanzania.
