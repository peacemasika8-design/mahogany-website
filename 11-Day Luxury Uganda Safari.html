Discover the True Heart of Africa

This carefully crafted 11-day luxury Uganda safari blends thrilling wildlife encounters, immersive cultural experiences, and breathtaking landscapes—from lush tropical forests to vast savannah plains. Explore some of Uganda's most iconic destinations, including Kibale National Park, Queen Elizabeth National Park, the remote Ishasha Sector, and the world-famous Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

To maximize comfort, this itinerary includes scenic domestic flights, reducing long travel hours and allowing you more time to enjoy each destination. Travel in style with Mahogany Travel Expeditions and experience Uganda's treasures in ultimate luxury.

Safari Highlights

  • Chimpanzee Trekking: Spend a magical hour observing chimpanzees in Kibale Forest
  • Gorilla Trekking: Life-changing encounter with mountain gorillas in Bwindi
  • Tree-Climbing Lions: Search for the famous Ishasha lions resting in fig trees
  • Kazinga Channel: Boat safari with hippos, crocodiles, and elephants
  • Cultural Experiences: Bigodi community visit and Batwa cultural encounter

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you'll be warmly welcomed by your safari guide and transferred to your luxury hotel. Relax and recover from your journey while enjoying views of Lake Victoria.

Accommodation: Protea Hotel Entebbe | Meal Plan: Dinner

Day 2: Journey to Kibale National Park

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Kibale, a primate paradise in western Uganda. Enjoy changing landscapes along the way before settling into your forest lodge.

Accommodation: Primate Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Chimpanzee Trekking & Bigodi Cultural Visit

Start your day with an exciting chimpanzee tracking experience in Kibale Forest. Spend a magical hour observing these intelligent primates in their natural habitat. In the afternoon, visit the Bigodi community for a cultural experience featuring traditional dances, coffee preparation, and local storytelling.

Accommodation: Primate Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Enjoy a relaxed morning before heading to Queen Elizabeth National Park. En-route, stop at the Equator for photos and a memorable experience. Arrive in time for a short game drive and a scenic boat transfer across the Kazinga Channel to your lodge.

Accommodation: River Station Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Experiential Game Drive & Community Visit

Cross to the mainland for a thrilling game drive in the Kasenyi plains—home to lions, elephants, buffaloes, and more wildlife. Have a lunch break and later, visit a nearby community for an authentic cultural interaction and storytelling session.

Accommodation: River Station Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6: Rwenzori Founders Art Center and Sculpture Gallery

Enjoy an early morning drive to Rwenzori Founders for art and craft experience, followed by a relaxing afternoon at the lodge.

Accommodation: River Station Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7: Transfer to Ishasha Sector

Travel south of Queen Elizabeth National Park to the remote Ishasha sector, famous for its rare tree-climbing lions. Enjoy scenic views along the way before checking into your wilderness camp.

Accommodation: Ishasha Wilderness Camp | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8: Tree-Climbing Lions Safari

Head out for a morning game drive in search of Ishasha's famous lions resting in fig trees. Enjoy a bush breakfast surrounded by nature before continuing your exploration.

Accommodation: Ishasha Wilderness Camp | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 9: Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

After breakfast, journey to Bwindi—home of the endangered mountain gorillas. Arrive in time to relax and prepare for the next day's adventure.

Accommodation: Nkuringo Bwindi Gorilla Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 10: Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Cultural Experience

Embark on a morning trek through the forest in search of mountain gorillas and spend a life-changing hour observing them. In the afternoon, visit the Batwa community to learn about their traditional forest lifestyle.

Accommodation: Nkuringo Bwindi Gorilla Lodge | Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 11: Scenic Flight to Entebbe

After breakfast, transfer to Kihihi Airstrip for a domestic flight back to Entebbe. Connect to your international flight or enjoy a day room depending on your schedule.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch
