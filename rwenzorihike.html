Let's Explore The Rwenzori Together!

Challenge yourself with a trek through the "Mountains of the Moon" with stunning alpine scenery and unique flora.

Duration: 6 Days / 5 Nights
Location: Rwenzori Mountains National Park
Max Altitude: 3,962m (Lake Bujuku)

6-DAY RWENZORI MOUNTAINS HIKING EXPEDITION

This 6-day hiking expedition takes you through the Rwenzori Mountains, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Experience diverse ecosystems from tropical rainforest to alpine moorland, with spectacular views and unique vegetation. The Rwenzoris, also known as the "Mountains of the Moon," offer some of Africa's most spectacular mountain scenery.

Difficulty Level:
Moderate to Challenging

Tour Highlights:

  • Trek through diverse ecosystems from rainforest to alpine zones
  • Hike to Lake Bujuku at 3,962m altitude
  • Spectacular mountain views including Margherita Peak (5,109m)
  • Unique giant lobelia and groundsel vegetation
  • Cultural encounters with Bakonzo people
  • Stay in mountain huts along the trail

Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL AND TRANSFER TO RWENZORI

Arrival in Uganda: Arrive at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to your accommodation in Kampala.

Briefing and Equipment Check: Evening briefing about the hike, equipment check, and meet your guide and porters.

Accommodation: Overnight at hotel in Kampala with dinner included.

DAY 2: TRANSFER TO KASESE AND START TREK

Travel to Kasese: Morning transfer to Kasese town at the foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains (approximately 5-6 hours drive).

Start Trekking: Begin your hike from Nyakalengija trailhead (1,600m) to Nyabitaba Hut (2,651m). Trek through tropical forest with chances of seeing primates and birds.

Accommodation: Overnight at Nyabitaba Hut with meals included.

DAY 3: TREK TO JOHN MATTE HUT

Morning Trek: Continue trekking from Nyabitaba Hut to John Matte Hut (3,414m). Cross the Kurt Shafer Bridge and hike through bamboo and montane forest.

Scenic Views: Enjoy views of Mount Baker and the surrounding peaks.

Accommodation: Overnight at John Matte Hut with meals included.

DAY 4: TREK TO BUJUKU HUT

Ascend to Bujuku Hut: Trek from John Matte Hut to Bujuku Hut (3,962m). Pass through the Bigo Bog and enjoy views of Lake Bujuku.

Alpine Zone: Enter the alpine zone with unique vegetation including giant lobelias and groundsels.

Lake Bujuku: Arrive at Bujuku Hut located near the beautiful Lake Bujuku with stunning mountain reflections.

Accommodation: Overnight at Bujuku Hut with meals included.

DAY 5: DESCENT TO NYABITABA HUT

Morning at Bujuku: Enjoy the morning scenery at Lake Bujuku before beginning your descent.

Return Trek: Trek back from Bujuku Hut to Nyabitaba Hut, retracing your steps through the different vegetation zones.

Accommodation: Overnight at Nyabitaba Hut with meals included.

DAY 6: FINAL DESCENT AND DEPARTURE

Final Descent: Trek from Nyabitaba Hut back to Nyakalengija trailhead.

Certificate Ceremony: Receive your Rwenzori hiking certificate at the park headquarters.

Return to Kampala: Travel back to Kampala with lunch en route.

Departure: Drop-off at your hotel or Entebbe Airport for your onward travel.

Tips & Recommendations

  • Fitness Preparation: This trek requires good physical fitness. Prepare with regular cardio exercise and hiking practice before your trip.
  • Packing Essentials: Waterproof hiking boots, warm clothing (layers), waterproof jacket and pants, gloves, hat, sleeping bag, headlamp.
  • Altitude Awareness: Be aware of altitude sickness symptoms. The trek allows for proper acclimatization, but listen to your body and inform your guide of any issues.
  • Weather Preparedness: The Rwenzoris are known for changeable weather. Be prepared for rain, cold, and possibly even snow at higher altitudes.
  • Best Time to Hike: December to February and June to August offer the best weather conditions for hiking.
  • Porters: Porters are available to carry the majority of your gear, allowing you to hike with just a daypack.
