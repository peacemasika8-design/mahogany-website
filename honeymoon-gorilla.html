About Mountain Gorilla Trekking

The Gentle Giants: Mountain gorillas are one of our closest living relatives, sharing about 98% of our DNA. There are only about 1,000 mountain gorillas left in the world, all living in the forests of Uganda, Rwanda, and DR Congo. Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is home to almost half of the world's population.

Gorilla Trekking Experience

Duration: 2-8 hours depending on gorilla family location
Time with Gorillas: 1 hour (strictly regulated for conservation)
Group Size: Maximum 8 visitors per gorilla family
Fitness Level: Moderate to challenging (guides assess fitness)
Age Limit: Minimum 15 years for gorilla trekking

Conservation Success: Thanks to tourism revenue and conservation efforts, mountain gorilla numbers have been increasing in recent years. Your visit directly contributes to their protection through park fees and community projects.

What to Expect During Your Trek:

  • Early Morning Briefing: Park rules, safety, and gorilla family assignment
  • Forest Trekking: Hiking through beautiful but challenging terrain
  • Tracking Team: Guides, trackers, and rangers ensure safety and success
  • The Encounter: Magical hour observing gorilla family behavior
  • Photography: No flash, maintain 7-meter distance, follow guide's instructions
  • Emotional Impact: Most visitors describe it as a life-changing experience
