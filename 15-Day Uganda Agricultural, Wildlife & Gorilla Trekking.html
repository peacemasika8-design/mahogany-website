The Ultimate Uganda Safari Experience

Uganda, famously known as the Pearl of Africa, offers an extraordinary combination of fertile agricultural landscapes, vibrant rural communities, diverse wildlife, and the rare opportunity to trek endangered mountain gorillas. This 15-day safari takes travelers through Uganda's most productive agricultural regions while also exploring the country's most iconic wildlife destinations including Queen Elizabeth National Park, Murchison Falls National Park, Kibale Forest, and gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Travel will be conducted in 4x4 safari vehicles with pop-up roofs, guided by experienced English-speaking safari guides from Mahogany Travel Expeditions.

Safari Highlights

  • Gorilla Trekking: Once-in-a-lifetime encounter with mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park
  • Chimpanzee Tracking: Trek through Kibale Forest to observe chimpanzees in their natural habitat
  • Murchison Falls: Game drives, boat safari on the Nile, and rhino tracking at Ziwa Sanctuary
  • Queen Elizabeth NP: Game drives on Kasenyi plains, Kazinga Channel boat cruise, and tree-climbing lions in Ishasha
  • Tea & Coffee Experiences: Kyaninga Tea Estate tour and coffee farming with local communities
  • Ankole Cattle: Visit the famous cattle corridor and learn about long-horned cattle farming
  • Batwa Cultural Experience: Meet the original forest inhabitants and learn their traditions
  • Lake Mburo NP: Game drives, nature walks, and zebra sightings

Detailed Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, meet a representative from Mahogany Travel Expeditions who will welcome you and provide a briefing about Uganda's agricultural sector and safari program. Transfer to your hotel for check-in and overnight.

Accommodation: Hotel in Entebbe

Day 2: Entebbe – Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary – Murchison Falls National Park

After early breakfast, drive north to Uganda's largest national park. Stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for guided rhino tracking on foot with trained rangers—the only place in Uganda where white rhinos can be seen in the wild. After tracking, enjoy lunch and continue to Murchison Falls National Park, passing through green vegetation and local communities. Arrive and check into your lodge.

Accommodation Options:

Luxury: Nile Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Sambia River Lodge | Budget: Gipir and Labongo Safari Lodge

Day 3: Morning Game Drive and Afternoon Boat Safari on the Nile

Embark on an early morning sunrise game drive with packed breakfast. This drive offers opportunities to see elephants, lions, giraffes, buffaloes, and antelopes. Enjoy a sunrise bush breakfast with beautiful views of wildlife and sounds from the falls. Return to the lodge for hot lunch and afternoon relaxation.

Afternoon Boat Safari on the Nile: Enjoy a boat cruise along the Nile River where you can see hippos, crocodiles, water birds, and elephants along the riverbanks.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Nile Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Sambia River Lodge | Budget: Gipir and Labongo Safari Lodge

Day 4: Murchison Falls – Fort Portal

Travel to the beautiful crater lakes region near Fort Portal. Agricultural Stopovers: Visit roadside farms producing bananas, cassava, maize, and vegetables. Interact with farmers to learn about small-scale farming techniques.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Primate Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Turraco Safari Lodge | Budget: Mountain of the Moon Hotel

Day 5: Tea Plantation Experience in Fort Portal

Visit the famous Kyaninga Tea Estate. Activities include walking through tea plantations, learning and participating in tea harvesting, visiting tea processing facilities, and participating in tea tasting sessions. Later, visit dairy farms to observe cattle management and milk processing.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Primate Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Turraco Safari Lodge | Budget: Mountain of the Moon Hotel

Day 6: Chimpanzee Tracking – Kibale National Park

Early morning breakfast, then set off through the tropical rainforest to track chimpanzees in their natural habitat. The forest is also home to red colobus monkeys, L'Hoest monkeys, and over 300 bird species. Return to the lodge for dinner, evening relaxation, and overnight.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Primate Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Turraco Safari Lodge | Budget: Mountain of the Moon Hotel

Day 7: Kibale – Queen Elizabeth National Park

Morning breakfast and drive to Queen Elizabeth National Park with agricultural stopovers. Visit Mubuku Irrigation Scheme farming projects including cassava farming, vegetable gardens, and other agricultural products. After the irrigation tour, continue to Queen Elizabeth National Park for check-in and overnight.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Mweya Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge | Budget: Acacia Wilderness Mweya

Day 8: Wildlife Safari in Queen Elizabeth National Park

Morning Game Drive: Early breakfast and set off for a game drive exploring the Kasenyi plains searching for lions, elephants, Uganda kobs, buffaloes, and over 600 bird species. Return to the lodge for lunch and afternoon relaxation.

Afternoon Boat Safari on Kazinga Channel: Cruise the channel connecting Lake George and Lake Edward with views of hippos, crocodiles, large elephant herds, and water birds. Return to the lodge for sunset relaxation, dinner, and overnight.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Mweya Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge | Budget: Acacia Wilderness Mweya

Day 9: Coffee Farming Experience

Visit rural communities near Queen Elizabeth National Park. Activities include walking through coffee farms, harvesting coffee beans with farmers, demonstration of coffee roasting and grinding, and coffee tasting experience.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Mweya Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Ihamba Lakeside Safari Lodge | Budget: Acacia Wilderness Mweya

Day 10: Queen Elizabeth – Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Early morning breakfast and drive through the Ishasha sector, famous for tree-climbing lions. Continue to Bwindi, home to nearly half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Nkuringo Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge | Budget: Bwindi Neck View Lodge

Day 11: Gorilla Trekking Experience

Early breakfast and set off for one of the most unforgettable wildlife experiences in the world—Gorilla Trekking. With experienced park rangers, hike through dense rainforest searching for mountain gorillas. Once found, spend one magical hour observing the gorillas as they interact, feed, and care for their young. Return to the lodge for lunch and afternoon relaxation.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Nkuringo Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge | Budget: Bwindi Neck View Lodge

Day 12: Batwa Cultural & Agricultural Experience

After breakfast, visit the Batwa community, the original forest inhabitants. Interact with them to learn about traditional forest survival techniques, visit rural farms run by Batwa communities, and enjoy cultural dances and storytelling.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Nkuringo Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge | Budget: Bwindi Neck View Lodge

Day 13: Bwindi – Lake Mburo National Park – Mbarara (Ankole Cattle Farms)

Early morning breakfast and travel through Uganda's cattle corridor. Visit the Ankole Cattle Farm raising the famous Ankole long-horned cattle. Farmers demonstrate traditional cattle keeping, milk production, and dairy processing.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Mihingo Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Kigarama Wilderness Lodge | Budget: Heyen Hills Safari Lodge

Day 14: Game Drive and Nature Walk – Lake Mburo National Park

Embark on an early morning game drive with packed breakfast in search of zebras, impalas, elands, and buffaloes. Enjoy a sunrise bush breakfast stop in the wild. Return to the lodge for lunch and afternoon relaxation. Later, enjoy a guided sunset nature walking safari to explore wildlife on foot.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Mihingo Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Kigarama Wilderness Lodge | Budget: Heyen Hills Safari Lodge

Day 15: Transfer to Entebbe – Departure

Return to Entebbe International Airport for departure. Final opportunities for souvenir shopping and farewell to the Pearl of Africa.

Meals: Breakfast
