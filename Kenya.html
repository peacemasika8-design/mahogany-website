Kenya Destinations

Kenya - The Classic African Safari

Kenya is where the modern safari was born and remains one of Africa's most popular wildlife destinations. The Maasai Mara National Reserve is world-famous for the Great Migration (July-October) when millions of wildebeest cross the Mara River. Kenya offers diverse ecosystems from the savannas of Amboseli with Kilimanjaro views to the lakes of the Great Rift Valley and the arid landscapes of Samburu.

Kenya's conservation success stories include the recovery of rhino populations in Lewa Wildlife Conservancy and the protection of elephants in Amboseli. The country offers excellent cultural experiences with Maasai and Samburu communities. Coastal destinations like Mombasa and Malindi provide beautiful beach extensions. Nairobi, the capital, offers unique attractions like the Giraffe Centre, Elephant Orphanage, and Nairobi National Park where wildlife can be seen with city skyscrapers in the background.

Explore Kenya's incredible destinations from world-famous national parks and wildlife reserves to pristine beaches, majestic mountains, vibrant cities, and rich cultural sites. Discover the Jewel of East Africa.
