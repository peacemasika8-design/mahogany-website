The Ultimate Uganda & Rwanda Safari Experience

This 15-day cross-border safari combines the best of Uganda and Rwanda—two of Africa's most spectacular destinations. From the mighty Murchison Falls and the primate capital of Kibale Forest to the mountain gorillas of Bwindi and the golden monkeys of Volcanoes National Park, this journey offers an unforgettable adventure through diverse landscapes, rich wildlife, and vibrant cultures.

Travel in comfortable 4x4 safari vehicles with pop-up roofs, accompanied by professional English-speaking guides from Mahogany Travel Expeditions who will ensure an immersive and seamless experience across both countries.

Safari Highlights

  • Murchison Falls: Sunrise game drives, rhino tracking at Ziwa Sanctuary, and boat safari to the base of the powerful falls
  • Kibale Forest: Chimpanzee tracking in the primate capital of the world and Bigodi Swamp Walk
  • Queen Elizabeth NP: Kazinga Channel boat cruise, evening game drives, and the famous tree-climbing lions of Ishasha
  • Bwindi Impenetrable Forest: Mountain gorilla trekking and Batwa cultural experience
  • Lake Bunyonyi: Relaxation at one of Africa's most beautiful lakes
  • Volcanoes National Park: Dian Fossey Research Center and Golden Monkey tracking
  • Nyungwe Forest: Canopy walk adventure and tea plantation experience
  • Kigali: Genocide Memorial and cultural heritage tour

Detailed Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival and Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park

Your adventure begins with an early morning departure after breakfast at 6:30 am. Enjoy a scenic drive to Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda's largest and most diverse park. On the way, stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for an exciting rhino tracking experience—your chance to see these incredible animals up close in their natural habitat. Have a lunch and refreshment break, then continue to the park and head straight to your lodge for a relaxing evening, dinner, and overnight stay.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Kulu Ora Lodge | Mid-range: Sambia River Lodge | Budget: Ikorom Safari Lodge

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 2: Sunrise Game Drive & Boat Cruise on the Mighty River Nile

Start your day early with a sunrise game drive across the savannah. Expect to encounter giraffes, elephants, lions, buffaloes, antelopes, and a wide variety of birdlife. Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation before heading out in the afternoon for a boat safari along the Nile. Cruise towards the base of the powerful Murchison Falls while spotting hippos, crocodiles, and more wildlife along the riverbanks.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Kulu Ora Lodge | Mid-range: Sambia River Lodge | Budget: Ikorom Safari Lodge

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 3: Journey to Kibale Forest National Park – The Primate Capital

After breakfast, set off for Kibale Forest National Park, famously known as the primate capital of the world. Enjoy a scenic drive through rural landscapes with a stop for lunch along the way. Upon arrival, check into your lodge and unwind in preparation for the next day's adventure.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Primate Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Turaco Treetops Lodge | Budget: Kibale Forest Camp

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 4: Chimpanzee Tracking & Bigodi Swamp Walk

Begin your day with an early briefing before heading into the forest for chimpanzee tracking. Spend time observing these fascinating primates in their natural habitat—an experience you won't forget. In the afternoon, explore the beautiful Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, known for its rich birdlife and unique ecosystem, as well as cultural encounters with the local community.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Primate Safari Lodge | Mid-range: Turaco Treetops Lodge | Budget: Kibale Forest Camp

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 5: Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

After breakfast, travel to Queen Elizabeth National Park, one of Uganda's most popular safari destinations. In the afternoon, enjoy a relaxing boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, famous for its high concentration of hippos, crocodiles, and birdlife. Later, head out for an evening game drive in search of lions, elephants, and other wildlife.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: River Station Lodge | Mid-range: Buffalo Safari Lodge | Budget: Acacia Wilderness Mweya

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 6: Experiential Safari & Sunset at the Twin Lakes

Today offers a more immersive safari experience with a guided game drive that brings you closer to nature. After lunch and some relaxation, head to the Twin Lakes for a magical sundowner. Enjoy stunning views as the sun sets over the savannah—perfect for photography and unforgettable memories.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: River Station Lodge | Mid-range: Buffalo Safari Lodge | Budget: Acacia Wilderness Mweya

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 7: Dive Into Ishasha Sector – Land of Tree-Climbing Lions

Travel to the southern sector of the park, Ishasha, famous for its rare tree-climbing lions. Spend the day exploring this unique area and keep your eyes on the fig trees—this is where lions love to rest.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Ishasha Wilderness Camp | Mid-range: Ishasha Jungle Lodges | Budget: Ishasha Pride Lodge

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 8: Transfer to Bwindi – Home of Mountain Gorillas

Enjoy a morning game drive before heading to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, home to the endangered mountain gorillas. Arrive at your lodge and relax as you prepare for one of the most powerful wildlife experiences on earth.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Silverback Lodge Bwindi | Mid-range: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge | Budget: Orugano Bwindi Lodge

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 9: Gorilla Trekking & Batwa Cultural Experience

Today is the highlight of your safari. After an early briefing, venture into the forest to track mountain gorillas. Spending time with these gentle giants in their natural habitat is truly life-changing. In the afternoon, visit the Batwa community to learn about their culture, traditions, and history.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Silverback Lodge Bwindi | Mid-range: Rushaga Gorilla Lodge | Budget: Orugano Bwindi Lodge

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 10: Transfer and Relaxation at Lake Bunyonyi

After breakfast, transfer to the stunning Lake Bunyonyi, known for its peaceful atmosphere and beautiful islands. Spend the rest of the day relaxing, enjoying the cool breeze, and exploring the lake.

Accommodation Options: Luxury: Arcadia Lodges | Mid-range: Lake Bunyonyi Rock Resort | Budget: Craterbay Cottages and Camp

Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 11: Cross to Rwanda – Culture & Conservation

After breakfast, cross into Rwanda and head to Volcanoes National Park. Visit the Dian Fossey Research Center to learn about gorilla conservation, followed by a cultural experience at the Gorilla Guardians Village.

Accommodation: De Vinci Lodge | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 12: Golden Monkey Tracking & Lake Kivu Escape

Track the playful golden monkeys in the forest before transferring to Lake Kivu. Relax by the lakeside and enjoy one of Rwanda's most scenic and peaceful destinations.

Accommodation: Malahide Paradise Hotel | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 13: Tea Experience & Nyungwe Forest

Enjoy a relaxed morning before heading to Nyungwe Forest National Park, one of Africa's oldest rainforests. In the afternoon, visit a tea plantation and learn about tea production while interacting with local communities.

Accommodation: Nyungwe Hilltop View | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 14: Canopy Walk Adventure

Start your day with the famous canopy walk, offering breathtaking views of the rainforest from above. Later, transfer to Butare for an overnight stay.

Accommodation: Credo Hotel | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 15: Culture & Departure

Visit the King's Palace Museum and later proceed to Kigali. Explore the Genocide Memorial for a meaningful historical experience before your departure.

Meal Plan: Breakfast
