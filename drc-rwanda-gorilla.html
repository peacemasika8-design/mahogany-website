7-DAY DRC-RWANDA CROSS-BORDER GORILLA SAFARI

Cross-Border Adventure Mountain Gorillas Virunga Volcanoes Primates Conservation

The Ultimate Gorilla Experience Across Two Countries

Embark on an epic 7-day adventure that takes you across the border between the Democratic Republic of Congo and Rwanda for a comprehensive gorilla trekking experience. This unique safari allows you to compare and contrast gorilla trekking in two different countries, trekking with different gorilla families in both Virunga National Park (DRC) and Volcanoes National Park (Rwanda). You'll witness the majestic Virunga Volcanoes from both sides of the border, experience different trekking conditions, and contribute to conservation efforts in both countries.

Trekking Difficulty:
Challenging (Two separate treks in different terrains, altitude 2,500-4,000m)
2 Countries
One unforgettable gorilla experience across DRC and Rwanda

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVE KIGALI, TRANSFER TO RUBAVU/RWANDA BORDER

Kigali Arrival: Arrive at Kigali International Airport, Rwanda.

Brief City Tour: Depending on arrival time, brief orientation of Kigali.

Scenic Drive Northwest: Journey through Rwanda's beautiful "Land of a Thousand Hills."

Lunch en route: Stop at a restaurant with panoramic views.

Arrive at Border Town: Reach Rubavu (formerly Gisenyi) on Lake Kivu at the Rwanda-DRC border.

Lodge Check-in: Check into your lakeside lodge with views of Lake Kivu.

Border Briefing: Detailed briefing about tomorrow's border crossing process.

Document Check: Ensure all travel documents are in order for DRC entry.

Welcome Dinner: Dinner at the lodge overlooking Lake Kivu.

Early Night: Rest well for the adventure ahead.

DAY 2: CROSS INTO DRC, VIRUNGA NATIONAL PARK ORIENTATION

Early Breakfast: Breakfast at the lodge with Lake Kivu views.

Border Crossing: Cross from Rwanda into DRC at the Grande Barrière border post.

Meet DRC Team: Meet your DRC guide and driver on the Congolese side.

Transfer to Goma: Short drive to Goma, the gateway to Virunga National Park.

Security Briefing: Essential safety and security briefing for DRC travel.

Lunch in Goma: Lunch at a restaurant in Goma with views of Lake Kivu.

Drive to Kibati: Journey to Kibati, the starting point for gorilla trekking in Virunga.

Lodge Check-in: Check into your eco-lodge near Virunga National Park.

Gorilla Briefing: Detailed briefing about tomorrow's gorilla trek in Virunga.

Volcano Views: Evening views of Mount Nyiragongo's glowing lava lake (if clear).

Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with stories from your DRC guide.

DAY 3: GORILLA TREKKING IN VIRUNGA NATIONAL PARK, DRC

Early Breakfast: Hearty breakfast before your DRC gorilla trek.

Transfer to Park Headquarters: Short drive to Virunga National Park headquarters.

Registration & Briefing: Official registration and trekking briefing.

Small Group Assignment: Groups of maximum 4 people for a more intimate experience.

Begin DRC Trek: Start your trek through Virunga's montane forest.

DRC Gorilla Encounter: One magical hour with mountain gorillas in DRC.

Photography: Capture these magnificent creatures in their DRC habitat.

Trek Back: Return hike through the forest.

Certificate Presentation: Receive your Virunga gorilla trekking certificate.

Return to Lodge: Return for relaxation and reflection.

Afternoon Options: Relax or optional short nature walk around the lodge.

Evening Discussion: Compare notes on your first gorilla experience.

Special Dinner: Celebration dinner for your DRC gorilla encounter.

DAY 4: RETURN TO RWANDA, TRANSFER TO VOLCANOES AREA

Leisurely Breakfast: Breakfast with final views of the DRC volcanoes.

Optional Morning Activity: Short walk or relaxation before departure.

Check-out: Depart from your DRC lodge.

Return to Goma: Drive back to Goma for border crossing.

Lunch in Goma: Lunch before crossing back into Rwanda.

Border Crossing: Cross from DRC back into Rwanda.

Meet Rwanda Team: Reunite with your Rwanda guide and vehicle.

Scenic Drive: Drive to Musanze (Ruhengeri) in Rwanda's Volcanoes region.

Lodge Check-in: Check into your lodge with views of the Virunga Volcanoes.

Rwanda Briefing: Briefing about tomorrow's Rwanda gorilla trek.

Comparison Discussion: Discuss differences between DRC and Rwanda experiences so far.

Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with volcano views.

DAY 5: GORILLA TREKKING IN VOLCANOES NATIONAL PARK, RWANDA

Early Breakfast: Breakfast before your Rwanda gorilla trek.

Transfer to Kinigi Headquarters: Short drive to Volcanoes National Park headquarters.

Registration & Briefing: Official registration and Rwanda trekking briefing.

Group Assignment: Assignment to gorilla family (groups of max 8 people).

Begin Rwanda Trek: Start your trek through Volcanoes National Park.

Rwanda Gorilla Encounter: One hour with mountain gorillas in Rwanda.

Photography: Capture gorillas in the Rwandan setting.

Trek Back: Return hike, comparing with DRC trek experience.

Certificate Presentation: Receive your Rwanda gorilla trekking certificate.

Return to Lodge: Return for well-deserved rest.

Afternoon Cultural Visit: Optional visit to Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village.

Dual Celebration Dinner: Special dinner celebrating both gorilla experiences.

Experience Comparison: Group discussion comparing DRC and Rwanda treks.

DAY 6: OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES & LAKE KIVU RELAXATION

Leisurely Breakfast: Relaxed breakfast after two days of trekking.

Morning Options (Choose One):

  • Golden Monkey Tracking: Trek to see endangered golden monkeys (extra cost)
  • Dian Fossey Grave Visit: Hike to Karisoke Research Center (extra cost, requires booking)

  • Community Visit: Visit local schools or projects supported by tourism
  • Relaxation: Simply relax at the lodge and enjoy the views

Lunch: Lunch at the lodge or during your chosen activity.

Afternoon Transfer to Lake Kivu: Scenic drive to Lake Kivu.

Lake Kivu Lodge Check-in: Check into your lakeside resort.

Lake Activities: Optional boat ride, swimming, or lakeside walk.

Sundowner: Enjoy sunset drinks overlooking Lake Kivu.

Farewell Dinner: Special farewell dinner celebrating your cross-border adventure.

Safari Reflections: Final group discussion and sharing of experiences.

DAY 7: RETURN TO KIGALI & DEPARTURE

Leisurely Breakfast: Final breakfast overlooking Lake Kivu.

Morning Relaxation: Time to enjoy the lake views and reflect on your journey.

Check-out: Depart from your lakeside lodge.

Scenic Return to Kigali: Drive back to Kigali through beautiful countryside.

Lunch en route: Stop for lunch at a restaurant with panoramic views.

Kigali Arrival: Arrive in Kigali by early afternoon.

Optional Activities: Depending on flight time:

  • Last-minute souvenir shopping at craft markets

Conservation Impact

By trekking in both countries, you're supporting gorilla conservation efforts on both sides of the border. This cross-border approach helps fund anti-poaching patrols, veterinary care, and community projects in both DRC and Rwanda, creating a more comprehensive conservation strategy for the entire Virunga ecosystem.

Unique Experience

Few travelers experience gorilla trekking in both countries. This dual experience gives you unique insights into different approaches to conservation, tourism management, and community involvement. You'll return home with a more complete understanding of mountain gorilla conservation in Africa.

Border Crossing Information & Requirements

Visa Requirements

Rwanda: Visa on arrival for most nationalities (check specific requirements). DRC: Visa required in advance. We assist with visa application process for DRC. East African Tourist Visa: Covers Rwanda and Uganda (not DRC).

Health Requirements

Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate required for both DRC and Rwanda. COVID-19: Check latest requirements for both countries. Malaria: Prophylaxis strongly recommended for both countries. Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation, especially for DRC portion.

Logistical Support

We handle all border crossing logistics including:

  • Pre-trip visa guidance and support
  • Coordination between DRC and Rwanda teams
  • Border facilitation and paperwork
  • Vehicle and guide changes at the border
  • Security updates and contingency planning

Essential Tips for Cross-Border Gorilla Safari

Border Crossing Preparation

Document Organization: Keep passports, visas, vaccination certificates easily accessible. Currency Preparation: Have small denominations of US dollars for visa fees if needed. Patience: Border crossings can take time - approach with patience and flexibility. Digital Copies: Have digital copies of all documents accessible on your phone.

Comparative Trekking Experience

DRC Trekking: Often more rugged, smaller groups, more "adventure" feel. Rwanda Trekking: Generally more structured, better trails, larger groups. Different Gorilla Families: You'll see different families with different dynamics. Guide Styles: Appreciate different guiding approaches in each country.

Health & Safety Across Borders

Medical Preparation: Consult travel clinic for DRC and Rwanda requirements. Travel Insurance: Ensure it covers both countries including medical evacuation. Altitude Awareness: Both treks are at high altitude - acclimatize properly. Security: Follow all security guidelines, especially for DRC portion.

Cultural Considerations

DRC Culture: More informal, French-speaking, different interaction styles. Rwanda Culture: More structured, English/French/Kinyarwanda, cleaner and more organized. Photography: Always ask permission before photographing people. Cultural Sensitivity: Respect different norms in each country.

Best Time for Cross-Border Gorilla Safari:

  • Dry Seasons (Recommended): June-September & December-February - easier trekking and border crossing
  • Wet Seasons (Consider): March-May & October-November - lusher vegetation, fewer tourists
  • Permit Planning: Book well in advance - need permits for both countries
  • Border Considerations: Check political/security situation before travel
  • Festival Times: Avoid major holidays when borders may be busier
