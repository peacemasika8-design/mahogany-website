The Big Five of Africa

🦁
African Lion

The apex predator of African savannahs, known for their social pride structure and powerful roar.

Best Places to See:

Maasai Mara, Serengeti, Queen Elizabeth NP

🐘
African Elephant

The largest land mammal, known for intelligence, complex social structures, and matriarchal herds.

Best Places to See:

Amboseli, Tarangire, Murchison Falls

🦏
Black Rhino

Critically endangered browser with prehensile lip, primarily found in protected reserves.

Best Places to See:

Ngorongoro Crater, Lake Nakuru, Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

🐃
Cape Buffalo

Formidable grazer living in large herds, considered one of Africa's most dangerous animals.

Best Places to See:

Serengeti, Queen Elizabeth, Lake Manyara

🐆
African Leopard

Elusive, solitary predator known for strength and ability to haul prey into trees.

Best Places to See:

Samburu, Serengeti, Queen Elizabeth NP

Detailed Safari Itinerary

🇰🇪
KENYA LEG (Days 1-4)

DAY 1: NAIROBI TO LAKE NAKURU

Arrival Nairobi: Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, meet your guide.

Drive to Lake Nakuru: Scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley with photo stops.

Afternoon Game Drive: Enter Lake Nakuru National Park for first game drive.

Rhino Spotting: Search for both black and white rhinos in this rhino sanctuary.

Flamingo Spectacle: View thousands of flamingos lining the lake shores (seasonal).

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your lodge overlooking the park.

Evening Relaxation: Sundowners overlooking the lake, dinner and overnight.

DAY 2: LAKE NAKURU TO MAASAI MARA

Morning Game Drive: Early morning drive for predator activity and bird watching.

Breakfast at Lodge: Full breakfast with views of the park.

Scenic Drive: Journey to the world-famous Maasai Mara National Reserve.

Maasai Village Visit: Optional cultural visit to a Maasai manyatta.

Enter Maasai Mara: Afternoon arrival, game drive en route to your camp.

Camp Check-in: Settle into your luxury tented camp.

Sundowner Game Drive: Evening drive with spectacular sunset views.

Campfire Dinner: Dinner under the stars with sounds of the African night.

DAY 3: FULL DAY MAASAI MARA

Full Day Safari: Full day exploring the Maasai Mara with packed lunch.

Great Migration: Witness river crossings if visiting during migration season (July-October).

Big Five Focus: Concentrated search for lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino.

Predator Action: Look for cheetahs, hyenas, and other predators.

Mara River: Visit the famous river crossing points.

Picnic Lunch: Lunch in the bush with wildlife viewing.

Afternoon Game Drive: Continue exploration of different ecosystems within the reserve.

Return to Camp: Evening return, relaxation before dinner.

Optional Activities: Hot air balloon safari (extra cost, pre-booking required).

DAY 4: MAASAI MARA TO SERENGETI

Morning Game Drive: Final game drive in Maasai Mara.

Breakfast at Camp: Farewell breakfast in the Mara.

Border Crossing: Transfer to the Tanzania border at Isebania.

Immigration Formalities: Complete border crossing procedures.

Enter Tanzania: Meet your Tanzanian guide, continue to Serengeti.

Game Drive to Camp: Afternoon game drive through Serengeti to your lodge.

Serengeti Arrival: Experience the vastness of the Serengeti plains.

Evening Relaxation: Sundowners, dinner and overnight in the heart of Serengeti.

🇹🇿
TANZANIA LEG (Days 5-8)

DAY 5: FULL DAY SERENGETI

Sunrise Game Drive: Early morning drive for predator activity and stunning sunrise.

Central Serengeti Exploration: Full day exploring different regions of Serengeti.

Kopjes: Visit granite rock formations that serve as leopard lookout points.

Great Migration Tracking: Follow the herds if in season.

Big Cat Country: Serengeti has Africa's highest concentration of large predators.

Picnic Lunch: Bush lunch with panoramic views.

Afternoon Safari: Continue wildlife viewing in different habitats.

Return to Lodge: Evening return, share stories of the day's sightings.

DAY 6: SERENGETI TO NGORONGORO

Morning Game Drive: Final Serengeti game drive with breakfast boxes.

Drive to Ngorongoro: Scenic drive with possible game viewing en route.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area: Enter this UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Crater Rim Viewpoint: Stop at viewpoints overlooking the crater.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your lodge on the crater rim with spectacular views.

Afternoon at Leisure: Relax and enjoy lodge facilities.

Conservation Talk: Evening presentation about Ngorongoro's unique ecosystem.

Dinner with a View: Dinner overlooking the crater.

DAY 7: NGORONGORO CRATER TO LAKE MANYARA

Crater Descent: Early morning descent into the Ngorongoro Crater.

Full Morning Safari: Game drive in this natural amphitheater with high wildlife density.

Black Rhino: Search for the endangered black rhino.

Big Five in One Place: Ngorongoro offers chance to see all Big Five in one location.

Lunch at Hippo Pool: Picnic lunch by a hippo-filled pool.

Afternoon Ascend: Drive back up the crater walls.

Transfer to Lake Manyara: Drive to Lake Manyara National Park.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at lodge near Lake Manyara.

Evening Relaxation: Dinner and overnight.

DAY 8: LAKE MANYARA TO UGANDA

Morning Game Drive: Game drive in Lake Manyara National Park.

Tree-Climbing Lions: Search for Lake Manyara's famous tree-climbing lions.

Bird Watching: Lake Manyara is a birdwatcher's paradise with over 400 species.

Transfer to Arusha: Drive to Arusha for lunch.

Flight to Uganda: Transfer to Kilimanjaro Airport for flight to Entebbe, Uganda.

Arrival Uganda: Arrive at Entebbe International Airport, meet Ugandan guide.

Hotel Transfer: Transfer to your hotel in Entebbe or Kampala.

Briefing: Evening briefing about Uganda leg of the safari.

🇺🇬
UGANDA LEG (Days 9-12)

DAY 9: TO MURCHISON FALLS NATIONAL PARK

Early Departure: Drive north to Murchison Falls National Park.

Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary: Stop for rhino tracking on foot.

Lunch en route: Stop in Masindi town for lunch.

Enter Murchison Falls: Afternoon arrival, game drive en route to lodge.

Nile River Views: Lodge located with views of the Nile River.

Evening Relaxation: Sundowners overlooking the river, dinner and overnight.

DAY 10: MURCHISON FALLS EXPLORATION

Morning Game Drive: Early game drive in the northern sector.

Wildlife Viewing: Look for lions, elephants, giraffes, and Uganda kob.

Return for Breakfast: Breakfast at the lodge.

Boat Cruise: Afternoon boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls.

Hippos & Crocodiles: See huge populations of hippos and Nile crocodiles.

Waterfall Hike: Optional hike to the top of the falls.

Evening Game Drive: Another game drive for different wildlife encounters.

Dinner at Lodge: Share stories of the day's adventures.

DAY 11: TO QUEEN ELIZABETH NATIONAL PARK

Scenic Drive: Drive south through Uganda's beautiful countryside.

Lunch en route: Stop in Fort Portal for lunch with mountain views.

Enter Queen Elizabeth NP: Afternoon arrival, game drive en route to lodge.

Crater Lake Drive: Scenic drive past volcanic crater lakes.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at lodge with views of the park.

Sundowner Game Drive: Evening drive looking for tree-climbing lions of Ishasha.

Farewell Dinner: Celebration dinner for your East Africa safari completion.

DAY 12: DEPARTURE FROM UGANDA

Morning Game Drive: Final safari drive in Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Breakfast at Lodge: Farewell breakfast in the bush.

Optional Activities: Choose from:

  • Boat cruise on Kazinga Channel
  • Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge (extra cost)
  • Community visit to local fishing villages

Transfer to Entebbe: Drive back to Entebbe with lunch en route.

Souvenir Shopping: Stop at craft markets for last-minute souvenirs.

Airport Transfer: Arrive at Entebbe International Airport for departure.

Farewell: Bid farewell to your guide and East Africa.

Extension Options: Add gorilla trekking in Bwindi or relaxation at Lake Bunyonyi.

Essential Safari Tips for East Africa

Best Time to Visit

Dry Seasons (Best): June-October & December-March for optimal wildlife viewing. Great Migration: July-October for river crossings in Maasai Mara. Wet Seasons: April-May & November - fewer tourists, lush scenery but some roads may be challenging. Year-Round: Ngorongoro and many parks offer good wildlife viewing year-round.

What to Pack

Clothing: Neutral colors (khaki, green, brown), layering for temperature changes. Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes and sandals. Essentials: Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, binoculars, camera with zoom lens. Health: Malaria prophylaxis, insect repellent, personal medications. Documents: Passport with 6+ months validity, visas for all three countries, vaccination certificates.

Border Crossings & Visas

East Africa Tourist Visa: Consider the joint visa covering Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda (Tanzania separate). Single Entry Visas: Can obtain visas on arrival for most nationalities. Documentation: Keep passport copies, yellow fever vaccination certificate. Crossing Times: Border crossings can take 1-2 hours - be patient. Currency: Have small amounts of local currencies for border purchases.

Health & Safety

Vaccinations: Yellow fever mandatory, hepatitis A&B, typhoid recommended. Malaria: High-risk areas - prophylaxis essential. Travel Insurance: Comprehensive coverage including medical evacuation. Water: Drink only bottled or purified water. Wildlife Safety: Always follow guide instructions, never approach animals.

Safari Vehicle & Accommodation

Vehicles: Customized 4x4 safari vehicles with pop-up roofs for game viewing. Accommodation: Mix of luxury lodges and tented camps with en-suite facilities. Meals: Full board accommodation - breakfast, lunch, and dinner included. Connectivity: Most lodges have Wi-Fi, but connectivity may be limited in remote areas.
