The Ultimate Ugandan Adventure

Experience the very best of Uganda on this carefully crafted 25-day journey combining iconic wildlife safaris, unforgettable primate encounters, and a full 7-day Rwenzori Mountains adventure, perfectly balanced with relaxation at Lake Bunyonyi. This itinerary is designed for travelers seeking both adventure and comfort, with a smooth flow and no rushed moments.

Travel in comfortable 4x4 safari vehicles with pop-up roofs for wildlife viewing, and experience the majestic Rwenzori Mountains with expert mountain guides. This expedition is led by professional English-speaking guides from Mahogany Travel Expeditions who ensure a seamless and unforgettable experience.

Expedition Highlights

  • 7-Day Rwenzori Mountains Trek: Complete mountain expedition through forest, bamboo, heather, alpine, and glacial zones
  • Murchison Falls: Sunrise game drives, rhino tracking at Ziwa, Nile boat safari, and Delta exploration
  • Kibale Forest: Chimpanzee tracking and Bigodi Wetland birding experience
  • Queen Elizabeth NP: Kazinga Channel boat cruise and Kasenyi plains game drives
  • Bwindi Impenetrable Forest: Double gorilla trekking experience with habituated families
  • Lake Bunyonyi: Three full days of relaxation, canoeing, and island exploration after trekking
  • Cultural Encounters: Community interactions and local experiences throughout

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival – Entebbe

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by our professional guide and transferred to your hotel. Enjoy a short safari briefing and relax after your journey.

Accommodation: Hotel in Entebbe

Day 2: Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park

Begin your adventure with a hot early breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Murchison Falls National Park, and have a stopover at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for rhino tracking. Have lunch and continue to Murchison Falls National Park, arriving in time for relaxation at your lodge.

Accommodation: Lodge in Murchison Falls

Day 3: Game Drive & Boat Safari

Start the day with an early morning game drive in search of lions, elephants, giraffes, and antelopes. Drive back to the lodge for early hot lunch. In the afternoon, enjoy a boat cruise along the Nile to the base of the falls, followed by a visit to the top of the falls for spectacular views.

Day 4: Nile Delta Exploration

Explore the Nile Delta (Delta Cruise) with wild places expertized safari tourist guide—ideal for bird lovers, with chances of spotting the rare shoebill stork. Optional evening game drive.

Day 5: Transfer to Kibale Forest

Depart for Kibale Forest through scenic landscapes dotted with tea plantations and crater lakes. Arrive and relax at your lodge.

Day 6: Chimpanzee Tracking

Enjoy an exciting chimpanzee tracking experience in Kibale Forest. Spend time observing these intelligent primates in their natural habitat. Afternoon at leisure or optional Crater Lake exploration.

Day 7: Bigodi Wetland & Cultural Experience

Take a guided walk in Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, known for rich birdlife and primates. Later, enjoy a cultural interaction with the local community.

Day 8: Transfer to Rwenzori Mountains Foothills

Drive to the foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains. Arrive early for briefing and preparation for the upcoming trek.

Rwenzori Mountains – 7-Day Trekking Experience

Conquer the legendary Mountains of the Moon through diverse ecological zones

Day 9: Trek Day 1 – Forest Zone

Begin your trek through dense tropical rainforest, home to unique wildlife and bird species.

Day 10: Trek Day 2 – Bamboo Zone

Ascend through bamboo forests with increasing altitude and changing vegetation.

Day 11: Trek Day 3 – Heather Zone

Enter the heather zone, characterized by giant plants and breathtaking views.

Day 12: Trek Day 4 – Alpine Zone

Explore the alpine landscape with dramatic scenery and rare vegetation.

Day 13: Trek Day 5 – Glacial Region

Reach higher altitudes with views of glaciers and rugged peaks.

Day 14: Trek Day 6 – Summit / Exploration

Optional summit attempt depending on fitness and conditions, or explore surrounding peaks.

Day 15: Trek Day 7 – Descent

Descend back to the base and transfer to a nearby lodge for relaxation.

Day 16: Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Proceed to Queen Elizabeth National Park. Enjoy a relaxed evening at your lodge or a short game drive.

Day 17: Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Safari

Morning game drive in the Kasenyi plains. Afternoon boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, famous for large concentrations of hippos, elephants, and birdlife.

Day 18: Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Travel to Bwindi through scenic highland landscapes. Arrive and prepare for gorilla trekking.

Day 19: Gorilla Trekking Experience

Embark on a once-in-a-lifetime gorilla trekking adventure. Spend one magical hour with a habituated gorilla family. Afternoon relaxation.

Day 20: Second Gorilla Trek / Forest Walk

Optional second gorilla trek for deeper experience or guided forest walk.

Day 21: Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi

Drive to Lake Bunyonyi, one of Africa's most beautiful lakes. Relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings.

Day 22: Lake Bunyonyi Relaxation

Enjoy canoeing, swimming, or exploring the islands. Perfect recovery after trekking.

Day 23: Lake Bunyonyi Leisure Day

Full day at leisure—ideal for relaxation or optional cultural visits.

Day 24: Lake Bunyonyi Relaxation

Another day to unwind, recharge, and enjoy the serene environment.

Day 25: Return to Entebbe & Departure

After breakfast, transfer back to Entebbe with a stop at the Equator. Continue to the airport for departure.

Meal Plan: Breakfast
