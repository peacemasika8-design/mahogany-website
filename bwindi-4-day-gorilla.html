Detailed Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN KIGALI & TRANSFER TO BWINDI IMPENETRABLE NATIONAL PARK

Upon your arrival at Kigali International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your Mahogany Travel Expeditions professional safari guide.

After a short briefing, begin your scenic drive through Rwanda's breathtaking "Land of a Thousand Hills" before crossing into Uganda via the Cyanika or Katuna border.

As you journey toward Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, admire rolling hills, lush tea plantations, picturesque villages, and stunning mountain landscapes.

Arrive at your luxury forest lodge in the afternoon or early evening, where you can relax and soak in the peaceful atmosphere of Africa's ancient rainforest.

Later in the evening, your guide will provide a detailed briefing about the Gorilla Habituation Experience, trekking etiquette, safety guidelines, and what to expect during tomorrow's adventure.

Accommodation: This will always depend on the region of Bwindi you are doing the trekking
Meals:

Lunch & Dinner

DAY 2: EXCLUSIVE GORILLA HABITUATION EXPERIENCE

Rise early for breakfast before heading to the park headquarters for registration and a comprehensive briefing from the Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers and conservation researchers.

Accompanied by expert trackers, researchers, and armed ranger guides, venture deep into the ancient rainforest in search of a mountain gorilla family currently undergoing habituation.

The trek may take anywhere from 2 to 6 hours, depending on the gorillas' movements.

Once the gorilla family is located, you'll spend an extraordinary four hours observing their natural behavior.

Watch playful juveniles tumble through the vegetation, silverbacks display their dominance, mothers nurse their young, and family members interact in their natural habitat.

Throughout the experience, researchers will share fascinating insights into gorilla behavior, conservation efforts, and the habituation process, making this one of the most immersive wildlife experiences available anywhere in the world.

Return to your lodge in the afternoon for relaxation and a well-deserved dinner.

Accommodation: This will always depend on the region of Bwindi you are doing the trekking
Meals:

Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

DAY 3: BATWA CULTURAL EXPERIENCE OR GUIDED FOREST WALK

Visit the indigenous Batwa people, the original forest dwellers of Bwindi. Discover their unique traditions, ancient hunting techniques, medicinal plant knowledge, music, dance, and storytelling while learning about their deep spiritual connection with the forest.

Have a lunch break and then explore another section of Bwindi with an experienced local guide for a Nature Walk, in search of colorful birds, playful primates, butterflies, and rare forest flora. Bwindi is home to over 350 bird species, including many Albertine Rift endemics.

Drive to Lake Bunyonyi for Relaxation on the lake. Enjoy showers at a private veranda, enjoy a spa treatment (optional) and in the evening, celebrate your unforgettable journey with a special farewell dinner.

What is Gorilla Habituation?

Gorilla habituation is the gradual process of helping wild mountain gorillas become comfortable with the presence of humans. This conservation effort takes approximately 2 to 3 years, during which researchers and trackers spend time with a selected gorilla family every day.

Unlike the standard gorilla trekking permit, which allows visitors to spend only one hour with a fully habituated gorilla family, the Gorilla Habituation Experience offers an exclusive opportunity to spend up to four hours observing these magnificent primates while learning directly from the experts involved in their conservation.

This rare experience provides a deeper understanding of mountain gorilla behavior, family dynamics, and the ongoing efforts to protect one of the world's most endangered species.

Essential Information & Tips

Gorilla Habituation Permits

Limited Availability: Only 4 habituation permits are available per day per gorilla family. Booking: Permits must be booked well in advance (6-12 months recommended). Cost: Habituation permits are more expensive than standard trekking permits due to the extended time and research involvement. Non-Transferable: Permits are issued for specific dates and are non-refundable.

Physical Preparation

Fitness Level: Moderate to good fitness required. Trekking can be strenuous with steep terrain and high altitude (1,160-2,607m). Training: Begin light cardio exercise several weeks before your trip. Pacing: Trek at your own pace - it's not a race. Porters: Consider hiring a porter to assist with your daypack.

What to Pack

Hiking Boots: Sturdy, waterproof boots with good ankle support. Layered Clothing: Moisture-wicking base layer, long-sleeved shirt, waterproof jacket. Gardening Gloves: For gripping vegetation during steep sections. Daypack: For water, snacks, camera, and rain gear. Camera: With extra batteries and memory cards.

Health & Safety

Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate required for entry to Uganda. Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation coverage. Age Limit: Minimum age is 15 years for habituation experience. Illness: If you have a contagious illness, you cannot trek (gorillas are susceptible to human diseases).

Best Time to Visit

Dry Seasons (Best): June-September & December-February - easier trekking conditions. Wet Seasons: March-May & October-November - lush scenery, fewer tourists. Year-Round: Gorilla habituation operates year-round.
