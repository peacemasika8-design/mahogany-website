Virunga Gorilla Adventure

This 3-day safari takes you to Virunga National Park in the Democratic Republic of Congo, Africa's oldest national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Home to about one-third of the world's remaining mountain gorillas, Virunga offers an incredible gorilla trekking experience amidst dramatic volcanic landscapes and lush rainforests.

Trekking Difficulty:
Challenging (steep terrain, high altitude)

Safari Highlights:

  • Gorilla Trekking: One-hour encounter with mountain gorillas in their natural habitat
  • Virunga National Park: Explore Africa's oldest national park (established 1925)
  • Volcanic Landscapes: Stunning views of the Virunga volcanoes including Nyiragongo
  • Biodiversity: Discover one of Africa's most biodiverse protected areas
  • Cultural Experience: Cross-border journey from Rwanda to the Democratic Republic of Congo
  • Conservation Support: Your visit directly supports gorilla conservation efforts

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: TRANSFER FROM KIGALI TO VIRUNGA NATIONAL PARK

Morning Departure: Pick up from your Kigali hotel or Kigali International Airport. Meet your guide who will brief you about the safari.

Scenic Drive to Border: Enjoy a picturesque 2-3 hour drive through Rwanda's beautiful landscape to the Rwanda-DRC border at Gisenyi/Goma.

Border Crossing: Complete border formalities with assistance from your guide (ensure you have DRC visa if required).

Transfer to Lodge: Drive to your accommodation near Virunga National Park headquarters in Bukima or Mikeno.

Evening Briefing: Receive a comprehensive briefing about gorilla trekking etiquette, safety procedures, and what to expect.

Accommodation: Overnight at lodge with dinner included.

Meal Plan: Dinner

DAY 2: GORILLA TREKKING IN VIRUNGA NATIONAL PARK

Early Morning Preparation: Wake up early for breakfast and pack your daypack with essentials (water, snacks, rain jacket, camera).

Park Headquarters: Transfer to the park headquarters for registration and briefing by park officials. You'll be assigned to a gorilla family and meet your ranger guides.

Gorilla Trekking: Begin your trek into the forest. The duration varies (1-4 hours) depending on the location of the gorilla family. The terrain can be steep and challenging at times.

Gorilla Encounter: Once the gorilla family is located, you'll spend one magical hour observing their natural behaviors - feeding, playing, social interactions, and possibly seeing silverbacks.

Return to Lodge: Trek back to the starting point and return to your lodge. Celebrate your gorilla encounter with fellow trekkers.

Optional Activities: In the afternoon, you can relax at the lodge or take a guided nature walk in the surrounding area.

Accommodation: Overnight at lodge with all meals included.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

DAY 3: RETURN TO KIGALI AND DEPARTURE

Morning Departure: After breakfast, begin your journey back to Rwanda.

Border Crossing: Complete exit formalities from DRC and entry back into Rwanda.

Scenic Return Drive: Drive back through Rwanda's beautiful countryside to Kigali.

Kigali City Tour (Optional): Depending on your flight schedule, you may have time for a brief Kigali city tour including:

  • Visit to the Kigali Genocide Memorial (highly recommended)
  • Craft market shopping for souvenirs
  • Lunch at a local restaurant

Airport Transfer: Arrive at Kigali International Airport for your departure flight, taking with you unforgettable memories of your Virunga gorilla experience.

Meal Plan: Breakfast

About Virunga National Park

Virunga National Park, established in 1925, is Africa's oldest national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Covering 7,800 square kilometers, it's home to an incredible diversity of habitats including active volcanoes, rainforests, savannas, and lakes. The park is most famous for its mountain gorillas but also hosts chimpanzees, okapis, forest elephants, and over 700 bird species. Virunga's conservation efforts have been instrumental in protecting mountain gorillas from extinction.

Essential Information & Tips

  • Gorilla Permits: Must be booked in advance. Virunga permits are often more available than Rwanda or Uganda permits.
  • DRC Visa: Required for most nationalities. Can be obtained in advance or at the border (check current requirements).
  • Fitness Level: Good physical fitness required. Gorilla trekking in Virunga can be challenging with steep, muddy terrain at high altitude.
  • Security: Check current travel advisories. Virunga has security measures in place for tourists.
  • Packing Essentials: Waterproof hiking boots, rain jacket, long pants, gardening gloves, insect repellent, camera with no flash.
  • Health Requirements: Yellow fever vaccination certificate required. Consider malaria prophylaxis.
  • Age Limit: Minimum age is 15 years for gorilla trekking.
  • Best Time to Visit: June-September and December-February (drier seasons).
