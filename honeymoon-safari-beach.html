Honeymoon Planning & Tips

Best Time for Honeymoon

Dry Season (Best): June-October for excellent wildlife viewing and beach weather. Migration Season: July-September for witnessing the Great Migration river crossings. Shoulder Season (Good Value): January-February & November-December - good conditions, fewer crowds. Green Season (Lush Landscapes): March-May - beautiful green scenery, occasional rain, lower prices.

Packing for Safari & Beach

Safari Clothing: Neutral colors, layers for temperature changes, comfortable shoes, sun protection. Beachwear: Swimwear, cover-ups, light cotton clothing, sandals. Evening Wear: Smart casual for dinners at luxury camps and resorts. Essentials: High SPF sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, camera with extra memory cards, binoculars.

Health & Safety

Vaccinations: Yellow Fever certificate required, malaria prophylaxis recommended. Travel Insurance: Comprehensive insurance including medical evacuation. Water Safety: Drink bottled water only. Sun Protection: African sun is strong - use high SPF sunscreen and reapply frequently.

Photography & Memories

Camera Equipment: DSLR or mirrorless with telephoto lens (70-200mm minimum) for wildlife. Memory Cards: Bring plenty - you'll take hundreds of photos. Backup: Daily backup of photos to cloud or external drive. Professional Photos: Consider booking a professional photoshoot to capture your special moments.

Budget & Extras

Inclusions: Most meals, accommodation, private safari vehicle, selected activities included. Extras: Balloon safari, premium drinks, optional excursions, spa treatments beyond included ones. Tipping: Budget for tips for guides, drivers, camp/lodge staff. Souvenirs: Local crafts, spices, artwork make wonderful memories.
