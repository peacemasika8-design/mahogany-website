Conquer the Mountains of the Moon

For travelers who dream of venturing beyond the ordinary, this remarkable journey offers an unforgettable opportunity to explore the mystical Rwenzori Mountains, famously known as the "Mountains of the Moon." Rising dramatically above the Albertine Rift Valley, these mountains are home to Africa's most spectacular alpine landscapes, crystal-clear streams, giant heather forests, and breathtaking views that few travelers ever witness.

After seven days immersed in one of Africa's most pristine mountain wildernesses, your adventure continues into the wildlife-rich plains of Queen Elizabeth National Park, where elephants, lions, buffaloes, hippos, and countless bird species roam freely across Uganda's most iconic safari destination.

This safari combines adventure, achievement, wildlife, and cultural discovery into one life-changing experience.

Safari Highlights

  • Rwenzori Mountains Trekking: Conquer the legendary "Mountains of the Moon" with experienced mountain guides
  • Alpine Landscapes: Experience giant heather forests, unique alpine vegetation, and breathtaking mountain views
  • Queen Elizabeth National Park: Game drives to see elephants, lions, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, and more
  • Kazinga Channel Boat Safari: Witness hippos, crocodiles, and abundant birdlife along the famous channel

Detailed Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe – Welcome to Uganda

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional guide and transferred to your hotel. Spend the remainder of the day relaxing and preparing for the adventure ahead.

Accommodation: Hotel in Entebbe | Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Day 2: Entebbe – Transfer to Rwenzori Mountains

After an early breakfast, depart for western Uganda on a scenic journey through lush countryside, rolling hills, tea plantations, and traditional villages. As you approach Kasese, the magnificent Rwenzori Mountains emerge from the horizon, their towering peaks often wrapped in clouds.

Accommodation Options:

High End: Snow Heights Safari Lodge | Mid-Range: Ruboni Community Camp | Budget: Turraco Campsite

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Begin the Rwenzori Trek

Your mountain adventure begins. Accompanied by experienced mountain guides and porters, enter the lower slopes of the Rwenzoris through dense tropical forests filled with birds, monkeys, giant ferns, and cascading streams. The fresh mountain air and pristine wilderness create an immediate sense of adventure.

Accommodation: Nyabithabha Camp | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Trekking Through Bamboo and Heather Zones

Embark on a second hike that takes you deeper into the mountain ecosystem. The vegetation changes dramatically as giant heathers, moss-covered trees, and unique alpine plants begin to dominate the landscape. Clouds drift through the valleys below while breathtaking mountain views unfold around every corner.

Accommodation: Jhon Matte Camp | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Explore the Heart of the Rwenzoris

Dive into one of the most rewarding sections of the trek. Navigate through beautiful valleys, crystal-clear streams, and dramatic mountain scenery that make the Rwenzoris one of the most unique mountain ranges on earth. Every step reveals landscapes unlike anywhere else in Africa.

Accommodation: Bujuko Camp | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6: High-Altitude Adventure & Summit Views

The higher elevations reveal a completely different world. Experience breathtaking views of rugged peaks, glacial formations, mountain lakes, and dramatic ridgelines that have inspired explorers for centuries. Standing amidst these legendary mountains is a truly humbling experience.

Accommodation: Elena Hut | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7: Descend Through the Mountain Forests

Begin your descent through changing ecosystems. As you move lower, the mountain reveals yet another face, with lush forests, colorful birds, and abundant plant life accompanying your journey. Celebrate your achievement upon returning to the mountain foothills.

Accommodation Options:

High End: Snow Heights Safari Lodge | Mid-Range: Ruboni Community Camp | Budget: Turraco Campsite

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8: Rwenzori Cultural Experience & Relaxation

After several adventurous days in the mountains, enjoy a more relaxed day exploring the culture of the Bakonzo people, the indigenous custodians of the Rwenzori region. Learn about traditional customs, folklore, farming practices, and the deep spiritual connection between the local communities and the mountains.

Accommodation Options:

High End: Snow Heights Safari Lodge | Mid-Range: Ruboni Community Camp | Budget: Turraco Campsite

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 9: Queen Elizabeth National Park – Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Safari

After breakfast, transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park. Enjoy an exciting game drive before embarking on the famous boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel. The change in scenery is remarkable as mountain landscapes give way to vast savannah plains teeming with wildlife.

Wildlife You May Encounter:

African Elephants Cape Buffaloes Uganda Kob Hippos Nile Crocodiles Waterbucks Lions Numerous Bird Species
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 10: Departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast, then embark on your return journey toward Entebbe for your departure flight or onward arrangements.

Meals: Breakfast
