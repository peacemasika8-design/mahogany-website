6-DAY SERENGETI WILDEBEEST MIGRATION SAFARI

Great Migration Serengeti Big Five Photography Balloon Safari

Nature's Greatest Spectacle

Embark on a 6-day safari adventure to witness one of the most awe-inspiring natural events on Earth - the Great Wildebeest Migration. Experience the dramatic river crossings, vast herds stretching to the horizon, and the constant cycle of life and death on the Serengeti plains. This safari is timed to maximize your chances of witnessing the migration's most dramatic moments, combined with exceptional year-round wildlife viewing in the world's most famous national park.

Safari Difficulty:
Easy to Moderate (Game drives with optional walking safari)

Tour Highlights:

  • Great Migration: Witness over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebra, and gazelle on their epic journey
  • River Crossings: Dramatic Mara River or Grumeti River crossings (season dependent)
  • Predator Action: Watch lions, cheetahs, and hyenas hunting amidst the migration
  • Hot Air Balloon Safari: Optional breathtaking aerial view of the migration (additional cost)
  • Big Five: Excellent chances to see lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino
  • Serengeti Ecosystem: Explore different regions of the vast Serengeti National Park
  • Bird Watching: Over 500 bird species including many raptors and migrants
  • Cultural Interaction: Visit to Maasai village (optional)

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: ARUSHA TO CENTRAL SERENGETI

Morning Departure: Pick up from your Arusha hotel or Kilimanjaro Airport. Briefing about the Great Migration and what to expect.

Scenic Drive: Journey through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area with possible stops at viewpoints.

Enter Serengeti: Arrive at Naabi Hill Gate, the main entrance to Serengeti National Park.

First Migration Sighting: Begin game driving immediately toward your lodge, searching for initial migration herds.

Lunch en route: Picnic lunch at a scenic location in the Serengeti plains.

Afternoon Game Drive: Continue toward central Serengeti (Seronera area) with wildlife viewing.

Accommodation: Check into your lodge or luxury tented camp in central Serengeti.

Welcome Dinner: Dinner with orientation about the migration patterns and wildlife behavior.

DAY 2: FULL DAY MIGRATION TRACKING

Sunrise Game Drive: Early morning departure to find the migration herds at their most active.

Migration Tracking: Follow the sounds and signs to locate massive herds of wildebeest and zebra.

Breakfast in the Bush: Picnic breakfast amidst the sounds of the migrating animals.

Predator Observation: Watch for lions, cheetahs, and hyenas following the herds.

Lunch at Lodge: Return to accommodation for lunch and midday rest.

Afternoon Migration Focus: Continue tracking migration movement patterns.

Photography Session: Optimal evening light for photography of the vast herds.

Sundowner: Drinks at a scenic viewpoint overlooking the migration.

Evening Talk: Presentation on migration ecology and conservation.

DAY 3: RIVER CROSSING DAY (SEASONAL)

Full Day River Focus: Depending on season, focus on either Grumeti River (May-July) or Mara River (July-October) crossings.

Early Positioning: Arrive early at crossing points to secure optimal viewing positions.

Crossing Watch: Patient observation as herds gather courage to cross crocodile-infested rivers.

Pack Lunch: Full day at crossing site with packed lunch and drinks.

Drama Unfolds: Witness the tension, panic, and survival of the crossing.

Crocodile Action: Observe Nile crocodiles during feeding opportunities.

Return to Lodge: Late afternoon return after a full day of migration drama.

Evening Reflection: Discuss the day's events and survival strategies.

DAY 4: HOT AIR BALLOON & NORTHERN SERENGETI

Balloon Safari Option: Pre-dawn departure for hot air balloon flight over the migration (optional, additional cost).

Aerial Views: Spectacular bird's-eye view of the vast herds from above.

Champagne Breakfast: Celebrate with champagne breakfast in the bush after balloon flight.

Travel North: Begin journey to northern Serengeti (Mara River region if in season).

En Route Game Drive: Full day game drive moving northward, following migration patterns.

Lunch on the Move: Picnic lunch during the journey.

Northern Arrival: Arrive at northern Serengeti accommodation by late afternoon.

Evening Game Drive: Short evening drive near your northern camp.

DAY 5: MARA REGION & PREDATOR FOCUS

Full Day Northern Exploration: Intensive game drives in the Mara River region.

River Crossing Points: Visit different crossing points along the Mara River.

Big Cat Tracking: Focus on finding lions, leopards, and cheetahs in the northern sector.

Migration Herds: Observe different aspects of migration behavior.

Bush Lunch: Picnic lunch at a scenic location in northern Serengeti.

Afternoon Options:

  • Option A: Additional river crossing observation
  • Option B: Focused photography session
  • Option C: Bird watching in riverine forest
  • Option D: Visit to Maasai village (additional cost)

Final Migration Evening: Last evening among the migration herds.

Farewell Dinner: Special dinner with migration celebration.

DAY 6: FINAL MORNING & RETURN TO ARUSHA

Final Game Drive: Early morning game drive for last migration sightings and photography.

Sunrise with the Herds: Experience sunrise amidst the waking migration.

Breakfast at Lodge: Final breakfast at your northern Serengeti accommodation.

Journey South: Begin return journey to Arusha with game drive en route.

Ngorongoro Viewpoint: Stop at Ngorongoro Crater viewpoint (weather permitting).

Lunch en route: Stop for lunch at a restaurant with views of the Rift Valley.

Cultural Market Stop: Optional stop at Maasai craft market (if time allows).

Arrival in Arusha: Drop off at your Arusha hotel or Kilimanjaro International Airport by late afternoon.

Extension Options: Combine with Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire, or Zanzibar beach extension.

About the Great Wildebeest Migration

The Greatest Show on Earth: The Great Migration involves over 1.5 million wildebeest, 200,000 zebra, and 300,000 Thomson's gazelle following an annual 1,200-mile clockwise circuit through the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem in search of fresh grazing and water.

Annual Cycle:

December-April: Southern Serengeti & Ngorongoro
Calving season - over 500,000 calves born in 2-3 weeks
May-July: Western Corridor & Grumeti River
Herds move west toward Grumeti River crossings
July-October: Northern Serengeti & Mara River
Dramatic river crossings into Masai Mara, Kenya
November: Returning South
Herds begin moving south as short rains begin

Predator-Prey Dynamics: The migration supports Africa's highest density of large predators. Lions, cheetahs, leopards, hyenas, and crocodiles all depend on the migration for survival, creating constant drama and natural selection.

Conservation Significance: The Serengeti ecosystem is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the oldest ecosystems on Earth, with climate, vegetation, and fauna largely unchanged for millions of years.

Essential Safari Tips for Serengeti Migration

  • Best Time for Migration Viewing:
    • Calving Season (Jan-Mar): Southern Serengeti - witness thousands of births
    • Grumeti River Crossings (May-Jul): Western Serengeti - dramatic river crossings
    • Mara River Crossings (Jul-Oct): Northern Serengeti - most famous crossings
    • Return Migration (Nov-Dec): Herds moving south with short rains
  • What to Pack for Serengeti:
    • Neutral-colored clothing (khaki, green, brown) for wildlife viewing
    • Warm layers for cool mornings and evenings
    • Waterproof jacket (rain can occur any time)
    • Sun protection (wide-brimmed hat, sunscreen, sunglasses)
    • Binoculars (essential for distant wildlife observation)
    • Camera with telephoto lens (minimum 300mm recommended)
    • Extra memory cards and batteries (limited charging opportunities)
    • Comfortable closed shoes for short walks
  • Photography Tips for Migration:
    • Telephoto lens (400-600mm) for wildlife close-ups
    • Wide-angle lens (16-35mm) for landscape and herd shots
    • Fast shutter speed (1/1000+) for action shots during crossings
    • Beanbag for vehicle window stabilization
    • Dust protection for camera equipment (Serengeti is dusty!)
    • Shoot in RAW format for maximum editing flexibility
  • River Crossing Etiquette:
    • Arrive early to secure good viewing position
    • Stay in vehicle at all times unless at designated viewing areas
    • Be patient - crossings can take hours to initiate
    • Listen to your guide's instructions about vehicle positioning
    • Respect other vehicles and photographers
  • Health & Safety:
    • Malaria prophylaxis essential
    • Yellow fever vaccination certificate required
    • Comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation
    • Stay hydrated - Serengeti can be hot and dry
    • Follow all safety instructions from your guide
