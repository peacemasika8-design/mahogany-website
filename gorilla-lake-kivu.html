Adventure and Relaxation in Rwanda

Experience the best of Rwanda on this 5-day safari that combines the thrill of mountain gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park with the serene beauty and relaxation of Lake Kivu. After the physical exertion of tracking gorillas through mountain forests, unwind on the shores of Rwanda's largest lake, enjoying water activities, scenic walks, and cultural experiences. This perfectly balanced itinerary offers both adventure and rejuvenation in Africa's cleanest and most beautiful country.

Discover Lake Kivu - Rwanda's Inland Sea

Africa's Sixth Largest Lake

Lake Kivu is one of Africa's Great Lakes, stretching 90km long and 50km wide at its widest point. It sits at an altitude of 1,460m, making it one of the highest lakes in Africa. The lake is shared between Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo.

Safe for Swimming

Unlike many African lakes, Lake Kivu is bilharzia-free and safe for swimming. The lake's unique methane gas layers prevent the snails that carry bilharzia from surviving, making it one of the few African lakes where you can swim safely.

Methane Gas Harvesting

Lake Kivu contains approximately 55 billion cubic meters of dissolved methane gas, which is being harvested for electricity generation. This unique feature makes the lake both a natural wonder and an important energy resource for Rwanda.

Island Paradise

The lake is dotted with beautiful islands, the most famous being Napoleon Island (home to a large fruit bat colony), Monkey Island (with vervet monkeys), and Amahoro Island (Peace Island). Boat trips to these islands are a highlight of any Lake Kivu visit.

Best Time for This Safari

Dry Seasons (June-September, December-February): Best for gorilla trekking with less mud and better trail conditions. Lake activities are also more pleasant. Wet Seasons (March-May, October-November): Lush green scenery, fewer tourists, but gorilla trekking can be more challenging. Lake Kivu is beautiful year-round.

Tips for Your Gorilla & Lake Kivu Safari

Combining Adventure & Relaxation

Pacing: This safari is designed to balance physical activity with relaxation. The gorilla trekking is moderately strenuous, while the Lake Kivu portion is for rest and leisure. Clothing Strategy: Pack both hiking gear for the volcanoes and casual, light clothing for the lake. Mindset: Be prepared to shift from adventure mode to relaxation mode as you move from volcanoes to lake.

Lake Kivu Activities

Water Safety: While Lake Kivu is bilharzia-free, always swim in designated safe areas and follow local advice. Boat Trips: Book boat trips through reputable operators and always wear life jackets. Island Visits: Some islands have specific visiting hours or requirements - check with your guide. Cultural Respect: When visiting fishing villages, be respectful of local customs and ask before taking photos.

Health Considerations

Altitude: Both Volcanoes National Park (2,500-4,500m) and Lake Kivu (1,460m) are at altitude. Stay hydrated and allow time to acclimatize. Sun Protection: Higher altitude means stronger UV rays - use sunscreen, hats, and sunglasses. Malaria: Prophylaxis is recommended for Rwanda, especially for lake areas. Travel Insurance: Ensure your policy covers medical evacuation and adventure activities.

Photography Tips

Dual Preparation: Your camera needs will vary from gorilla trekking (fast shutter, zoom lens) to lake scenery (wide angle, landscape settings). Water Protection: Bring waterproof bags or cases for your camera during boat trips. Charging: Ensure you have adequate charging opportunities between locations. Memory: Bring plenty of memory cards - you'll want photos of both gorillas and lake scenery.
