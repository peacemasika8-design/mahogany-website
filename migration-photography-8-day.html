Professional Photography Tips for the Migration

Camera Settings for Action

Shutter Priority Mode: Use Tv/S mode with minimum 1/1000s for walking animals, 1/2000s+ for running/jumping. Auto ISO: Set maximum ISO based on your camera's capability (6400-12800 for modern cameras). Continuous Focus: AI Servo/AF-C with focus point expansion.

Composition Techniques

Rule of Thirds: Place animals at intersection points. Leading Lines: Use riverbanks, paths, or herd lines to lead eye. Negative Space: Leave space in direction animal is moving. Eye Level: Shoot at animal's eye level for intimate connection.

Light Management

Golden Hours: Shoot sunrise to 2 hours after, and 2 hours before to sunset. Backlighting: Create rim light and dramatic silhouettes. Overcast Days: Perfect for even lighting and detail. Midday Challenge: Use for black and white or creative techniques.

River Crossing Specifics

Pre-focus: Focus on crossing point before action starts. Burst Mode: 10+ fps to capture every moment. Sequence Planning: Shoot wide establishing shot, medium action, tight details. Panning: 1/60-1/125s shutter for motion blur background.

Post-Processing Workflow

Daily Download: Download and backup images every evening. Keywording: Tag images immediately while memory is fresh. Selects First: Flag or rate images before any editing. Consistent Presets: Develop personal presets for efficiency.

Advanced Techniques Covered:

  • Focus Stacking: For maximum depth of field in landscape shots
  • Exposure Blending: For high dynamic range scenes
  • Time-lapse Sequences: Capturing herd movement over time
  • Panoramic Stitching: For ultra-wide landscape images
  • Multiple Exposure: Creative in-camera techniques
  • Intentional Camera Movement: Artistic blur effects
  • Black and White Conversion: Advanced toning techniques
  • Selective Color: Creative color manipulation
