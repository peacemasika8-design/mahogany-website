5-DAY GORILLA TREKKING & NYIRAGONGO HIKE COMBO

Ultimate Combo Gorillas Volcano Adventure DRC

Two Epic Adventures in One Unforgettable Trip

Experience the ultimate Democratic Republic of Congo adventure with this 5-day combo package that brings together two of Africa's most extraordinary wildlife and geological experiences. First, trek through the misty forests of Virunga National Park to encounter endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat. Then, challenge yourself with a hike to the summit of Mount Nyiragongo to witness the world's largest lava lake glowing in the darkness. This combination offers the perfect balance of primate encounters and volcanic adventure, making it one of the most sought-after experiences in Africa for true adventure seekers.

Combo Adventure Highlights:

  • Mountain Gorilla Encounter: One hour with endangered mountain gorillas
  • Nyiragongo Lava Lake: Overnight at crater rim with world's largest lava lake
  • Virunga National Park: Africa's oldest national park, UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Professional Guides: Expert guides for both gorilla trekking and volcano hiking
  • Conservation Support: Your visit directly supports gorilla and park conservation
  • Physical Challenge: Two different but equally rewarding physical adventures
  • Cultural Experience: Interaction with local communities around Virunga
  • Photography Paradise: Unparalleled wildlife and geological photography

Detailed Combo Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN GOMA & GORILLA BRIEFING

Border Crossing: Collection from Gisenyi, Rwanda or Goma International Airport.

Welcome to DRC: Transfer to your hotel in Goma with views of Lake Kivu.

Gorilla Trekking Briefing: Comprehensive briefing about tomorrow's gorilla trekking.

Meet Your Guide: Introduction to your gorilla trekking guide.

Equipment Check: Ensure you have appropriate clothing and gear for gorilla trekking.

Health Requirements: Briefing about health precautions for gorilla trekking.

Explore Goma: Optional short tour of Goma town if time permits.

Lake Kivu Views: Enjoy sunset views over Lake Kivu from your hotel.

Welcome Dinner: Dinner at hotel with opportunity to ask questions.

Early Night: Rest well for tomorrow's early start and gorilla trekking.

Packing Preparation: Prepare daypack for gorilla trekking.

DAY 2: MOUNTAIN GORILLA TREKKING

Early Breakfast: Hearty breakfast before departure to park headquarters.

Transfer to Bukima: Drive to Bukima Patrol Post - starting point for gorilla trekking.

Registration & Briefing: Official registration and final briefing by park rangers.

Group Assignment: Assignment to gorilla family group based on fitness level.

Meet Trackers: Introduction to trackers who located the gorillas earlier.

Begin Trekking: Enter the forest and start your gorilla trek.

Forest Trek: 1-4 hours of hiking through beautiful montane forest.

Gorilla Encounter: One magical hour with a mountain gorilla family.

Photography Time: Opportunity to photograph these magnificent creatures.

Trek Back: Return hike to starting point (typically 1-2 hours).

Certificate Presentation: Receive your gorilla trekking certificate.

Return to Goma: Transfer back to your hotel in Goma.

Afternoon Relaxation: Time to relax and recover from the trek.

Gorilla Trek Debrief: Share experiences and photos with fellow trekkers.

Nyiragongo Briefing: Evening briefing about the volcano hike.

Celebration Dinner: Special dinner to celebrate your gorilla encounter.

DAY 3: TRANSFER & NYIRAGONGO PREPARATION

Leisurely Breakfast: Breakfast at hotel with time to process gorilla experience.

Gear Check: Detailed equipment check for Nyiragongo hike.

Meet Volcano Guide: Introduction to your Nyiragongo hiking guide.

Porter Allocation: Porter assigned to carry your overnight gear (10kg limit).

Lunch in Goma: Energy-packed lunch before starting volcano hike.

Transfer to Kibati: Drive to Kibati Patrol Post (1,980m) - starting point.

Park Registration: Complete Virunga National Park entry formalities.

Final Preparations: Last-minute adjustments to gear and packing.

Begin Ascent: Start hiking through montane forest at 2:00 PM.

Forest Section: Initial trek through lush rainforest.

First Rest Stop: Break at 2,480m with views of Goma and Lake Kivu.

Continue Climbing: Steady ascent through changing vegetation zones.

Volcanic Landscape: Enter the volcanic zone with unique flora.

Arrival at Summit: Reach crater rim (3,470m) around 6:00-7:00 PM.

First Lava Lake View: Breathtaking first view of the lava lake!

Cabin Assignment: Settle into basic summit cabins (shared accommodation).

Dinner at Summit: Hot dinner prepared by the guide team.

Evening Lava Viewing: Spend evening watching the spectacular lava lake.

DAY 4: NYIRAGONGO DESCENT & LAKE KIVU

Volcano Sunrise: Wake for sunrise views of the lava lake.

Morning Spectacle: See the lava lake in morning light.

Summit Breakfast: Breakfast with the most dramatic view imaginable.

Final Photos: Last opportunity for photos of the lava lake.

Begin Descent: Start descent around 8:00 AM.

Volcanic Descent: Careful descent through steep volcanic scree.

Scree Running: Option to "scree run" down soft volcanic ash sections.

Re-enter Forest: Return to the cooler montane forest zone.

Arrive at Kibati: Complete hike at starting point around 1:00 PM.

Certificate Presentation: Receive your Nyiragongo hiking certificate.

Tip Your Team: Opportunity to thank guide and porters.

Transfer to Lake Kivu: Drive to Lake Kivu resort.

Hotel Check-in: Check into comfortable lakeside accommodation.

Well-Deserved Rest: Time to relax and recover from two big adventures.

Lake Swim: Optional swim in Lake Kivu (bilharzia-free).

Celebration Dinner: Special dinner to celebrate both achievements.

Relaxation Evening: Relax by the lake, share stories, process experiences.

DAY 5: LAKE KIVU & DEPARTURE

Leisurely Breakfast: Breakfast with beautiful Lake Kivu views.

Morning Options: Choose from several relaxing activities:

  • Boat Trip: Scenic boat trip on Lake Kivu
  • Island Visit: Visit to Napoleon Island (bat island)
  • Beach Relaxation: Relax on Lake Kivu beaches
  • Cultural Visit: Visit local fishing communities
  • Spa Treatment: Optional massage or spa treatment

Late Check-out: Extended check-out time for relaxation.

Farewell Lunch: Final lunch at Lake Kivu resort.

Photo Review: Time to review and share photos from both adventures.

Trip Debrief: Share thoughts and experiences about the combo adventure.

Transfer to Border/Airport: Drive to Goma International Airport or Rwanda border.

Border Formalities: Assistance with exit formalities if crossing to Rwanda.

Farewell: Bid farewell to your guide and driver.

Extension Options: Add more days for:

  • Additional gorilla trekking (different family)
  • Chimpanzee trekking in Virunga
  • Lowland gorilla trekking (Kahuzi-Biega)
  • Rwanda gorilla trekking extension
  • Uganda safari extension

Why This Combo is the Ultimate DRC Adventure

Perfect Physical & Emotional Balance

This combo offers the perfect balance between two different types of adventure. Gorilla trekking is more about emotional connection and wildlife appreciation, while Nyiragongo is about physical challenge and geological wonder. The sequencing allows for recovery between activities, with the gorilla trek first (moderate difficulty) followed by the volcano hike (more challenging).

Maximum Experience in Minimum Time

For travelers with limited time, this combo packs two of Africa's most extraordinary experiences into just 5 days. Instead of making two separate trips to the DRC, you can experience both highlights in one efficient itinerary. The proximity of gorilla habitats to Nyiragongo makes this combination logistically perfect.

Conservation Impact Multiplier

By participating in both activities, you're supporting multiple conservation initiatives in Virunga National Park. Gorilla trekking fees fund anti-poaching patrols and community development, while volcano hiking fees support geological monitoring and park infrastructure. Your combined contribution has a greater conservation impact.

Storytelling Perfection

This combination creates the ultimate travel story - from the intimate, emotional experience of meeting our closest relatives in the animal kingdom to the awe-inspiring, primal power of the Earth's interior. Few travel experiences can match the narrative power of gorillas one day, active volcano the next.

Physical Fitness Requirements

This combo requires good physical fitness. You'll need to complete two challenging activities back-to-back: gorilla trekking (2-6 hours hiking) followed by Nyiragongo (5-7 hours uphill with overnight at altitude). While there's a recovery day between them, you should be in good hiking shape. We recommend training with back-to-back hiking days before your trip. Minimum age is 15 for gorilla trekking, but we recommend 18+ for this combo due to the cumulative physical demands.

Essential Tips for Combo Adventure

Training & Preparation

Back-to-Back Training: Practice hiking consecutive days. Altitude Prep: Consider arriving early to acclimatize. Gear Testing: Test all equipment before trip. Medical Check: Ensure fitness for both activities.

Between Activities

Recovery Focus: Use Day 3 for rest and preparation. Hydration: Rehydrate thoroughly after gorilla trek. Nutrition: Eat well to rebuild energy stores. Gear Reorganization: Repack efficiently for volcano hike.

Photography Strategy

Different Settings: Gorillas (fast shutter, no flash) vs volcano (tripod, long exposure). Battery Management: Cold drains batteries on volcano. Memory Cards: Bring plenty for both activities. Protection: Protect camera from forest moisture and volcanic dust.

Health & Safety

Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate mandatory for DRC. Malaria: Prophylaxis recommended. Travel Insurance: Must include emergency evacuation. Altitude Awareness: Know symptoms for both activities.

Best Time for Combo Adventure:

  • Dry Seasons (Best): June-September & December-February
  • Gorilla Visibility: Dry seasons offer better gorilla viewing
  • Volcano Conditions: Dry trails make volcano hike easier
  • Clear Views: December-February often has clearest views
  • Avoid Heavy Rains: March-May & October-November can be challenging
  • Year-Round: Possible year-round with proper preparation
