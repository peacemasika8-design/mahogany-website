Overview

Experience gorilla trekking and visit Dian Fossey's tomb in Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park. This safari combines wildlife adventure with conservation history.

Trekking Difficulty

Challenging

Best Time

June-February

Gorilla Increase

54% since 1980s

Group Size

Max 8 per gorilla family

Key Highlights

  • Gorilla trekking (1 hour with gorillas)
  • Visit Dian Fossey's tomb at Karisoke
  • Learn about conservation history
  • Optional cultural village visit
  • Volcano views and photography

Brief Itinerary

DAY 1: KIGALI TO VOLCANOES

Arrival in Kigali. Transfer to Volcanoes National Park. Lodge check-in. Evening briefing.

DAY 2: GORILLA TREKKING

Morning gorilla trekking. 1 hour with gorillas. Afternoon relaxation. Certificate presentation.

DAY 3: FOSSEY TOMB VISIT

Hike to Dian Fossey's tomb at Karisoke Research Center. Historical briefing. Return to lodge.

DAY 4: OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES

Choose: Golden monkey tracking, cultural village, or relaxation. Traditional dance performance.

DAY 5: RETURN TO KIGALI

Morning departure. Optional Kigali city tour. Airport transfer for departure.

Quick Tips

  • Book permits 6+ months in advance
  • Gorilla permit: $1,500 per person
  • Fossey tomb permit: $75 per person
  • Good hiking boots required
  • No flash photography with gorillas

What to Pack

  • Waterproof hiking boots
  • Rain jacket and layers
  • Camera (no flash)
  • Sun protection
  • Basic first aid kit
