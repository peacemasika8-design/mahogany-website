Detailed Safari Itinerary

🦒
PHASE 1: NORTHERN TANZANIA SAFARI (DAYS 1-7)

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN KILIMANJARO - ARUSHA

Arrival: Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), meet your guide.

Transfer: Drive to Arusha town (approximately 1 hour).

Briefing: Comprehensive safari briefing with your guide.

Accommodation: Check into your Arusha hotel/lodge.

Rest & Acclimatize: Relax after your journey, adjust to Tanzania time.

Optional Activities: Depending on arrival time:

  • Visit Arusha National Park (extra cost)
  • Explore Arusha town and local markets
  • Relax at hotel pool and gardens

Welcome Dinner: Dinner at hotel, meet fellow travelers if in group safari.

Preparation: Final packing for safari, early night for tomorrow's start.

DAY 2: ARUSHA TO TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK

Morning Departure: After breakfast, depart Arusha for Tarangire.

Scenic Drive: Drive through Maasai steppe with views of Mount Meru.

Enter Tarangire: Arrive at Tarangire National Park gate.

Game Drive: Full day game drive in Tarangire National Park.

Baobab Trees: See the park's famous ancient baobab trees.

Elephant Paradise: Tarangire has one of Africa's largest elephant populations.

Lunch: Picnic lunch at scenic viewpoint in the park.

Afternoon Game Drive: Continue wildlife viewing, look for lions, leopards.

Lodge Arrival: Arrive at your Tarangire lodge for sunset.

Evening: Sundowners, dinner, overnight at lodge.

Night Sounds: Listen to sounds of the African night from your lodge.

DAY 3: TARANGIRE TO LAKE MANYARA

Morning Game Drive: Early morning game drive in Tarangire.

Breakfast: Return to lodge for full breakfast.

Transfer: Drive to Lake Manyara National Park.

Enter Lake Manyara: Arrive at Lake Manyara National Park.

Game Drive: Afternoon game drive in Lake Manyara.

Tree-Climbing Lions: Search for Lake Manyara's famous tree-climbing lions.

Bird Paradise: Lake Manyara is a birdwatcher's paradise with over 400 species.

Hot Springs: Visit the Maji Moto hot springs.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your Lake Manyara lodge.

Evening: Sundowners with views of the Rift Valley escarpment, dinner.

Cultural Option: Optional visit to local Maasai village (extra cost).

DAY 4: LAKE MANYARA TO SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

Morning Game Drive: Early game drive in Lake Manyara.

Breakfast: Return to lodge for breakfast.

Drive to Serengeti: Scenic drive via Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Ngorongoro Viewpoint: Stop at viewpoint overlooking the crater.

Enter Serengeti: Arrive at Serengeti National Park via Naabi Hill Gate.

Game Drive to Lodge: Afternoon game drive en route to your Serengeti lodge.

First Serengeti Impressions: Experience the vastness of the Serengeti plains.

Migration Tracking: Begin searching for the Great Migration herds (seasonal).

Lodge Arrival: Arrive at your Serengeti lodge in Central Serengeti.

Evening: Sundowners, dinner, overnight at lodge.

Night Game Drive Option: Optional night game drive if at specific lodges (extra cost).

DAY 5: FULL DAY SERENGETI EXPLORATION

Sunrise Game Drive: Early morning drive for predator activity and beautiful light.

Full Day Safari: Full day exploring different regions of Serengeti with packed lunch.

Migration Focus: Track the Great Migration herds (seasonal).

River Crossings: If in Northern Serengeti (July-October), witness dramatic river crossings.

Kopjes Exploration: Visit granite rock formations that serve as leopard lookout points.

Picnic Lunch: Bush lunch at scenic location in the park.

Afternoon Game Drive: Continue wildlife viewing in different habitats.

Retirement Home: Visit Seronera River area, excellent for wildlife.

Return to Lodge: Evening return to lodge.

Optional Activities: Hot air balloon safari (extra cost, pre-booking required).

Evening: Dinner, share stories of the day's sightings.

DAY 6: SERENGETI TO NGORONGORO CRATER

Morning Game Drive: Final Serengeti game drive with breakfast boxes.

Farewell to Serengeti: Last wildlife viewing in Serengeti.

Drive to Ngorongoro: Scenic drive to Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Crater Rim Views: Stop at viewpoints overlooking the crater.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your Ngorongoro Crater rim lodge.

Afternoon at Leisure: Relax at lodge with spectacular crater views.

Optional Activities:

  • Visit to Olduvai Gorge museum (extra cost)
  • Walk along crater rim with guide
  • Relax at lodge, enjoy facilities

Crater Briefing: Evening briefing about tomorrow's crater descent.

Dinner with a View: Dinner overlooking the crater.

DAY 7: NGORONGORO CRATER TO ARUSHA

Crater Descent: Early morning descent into Ngorongoro Crater.

Full Morning Safari: Game drive in the crater - world's largest intact volcanic caldera.

Big Five in One Place: Excellent chance to see all Big Five in one morning.

Black Rhino: Search for the endangered black rhino.

Hippo Pool: Visit hippo-filled pools for photography.

Lunch: Picnic lunch at scenic spot in the crater.

Afternoon Ascend: Drive back up the crater walls.

Return to Arusha: Drive back to Arusha (approximately 3-4 hours).

Arrival: Arrive in Arusha by late afternoon.

Hotel Check-in: Check into Arusha hotel for overnight.

Farewell Dinner: Celebration dinner for completed safari portion.

Preparation: Pack for beach portion, early night for flight tomorrow.

🏖️
PHASE 2: ZANZIBAR BEACH HOLIDAY (DAYS 8-12)

DAY 8: ARUSHA TO ZANZIBAR - STONE TOWN

Morning Transfer: Transfer to Arusha Airport or Kilimanjaro International Airport.

Flight to Zanzibar: Fly to Zanzibar (approximately 1.5 hours).

Arrival Zanzibar: Arrive at Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ).

Meet Representative: Meet your Zanzibar representative/guide.

Transfer to Stone Town: Drive to historic Stone Town (approximately 20 minutes).

Hotel Check-in: Check into your Stone Town hotel.

Stone Town Tour: Afternoon guided tour of Stone Town including:

  • Former Slave Market and Anglican Cathedral
  • House of Wonders (Beit-al-Ajaib)
  • Old Fort (Ngome Kongwe)
  • Forodhani Gardens
  • Darajani Market
  • Freddie Mercury House (optional)

Evening: Dinner at Stone Town restaurant, overnight at hotel.

Night Market: Visit Forodhani Night Market for local street food (optional).

DAY 9: SPICE TOUR & TRANSFER TO BEACH

Morning: Breakfast at hotel.

Spice Tour: Half-day spice plantation tour - discover why Zanzibar is called the "Spice Island".

Spice Demonstration: See, smell, and taste various spices:

  • Cloves (Zanzibar's most famous spice)
  • Nutmeg, cinnamon, black pepper
  • Vanilla, cardamom, turmeric
  • Tropical fruits: jackfruit, breadfruit, etc.

Lunch: Traditional Swahili lunch at spice farm.

Transfer to Beach: Afternoon transfer to your beach resort.

Beach Areas: Depending on choice: Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Jambiani, or Matemwe.

Resort Check-in: Check into your beach resort.

Beach Time: First experience of Zanzibar's famous beaches.

Sunset: Spectacular Indian Ocean sunset.

Evening: Dinner at resort, overnight.

DAY 10: BEACH RELAXATION & OPTIONAL ACTIVITIES

Leisurely Morning: Breakfast at resort, day at leisure.

Beach Options:

  • Relaxation: Sunbathe, swim, read, complete relaxation
  • Water Sports: Kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding (often included)
  • Snorkeling: Half-day snorkeling trip to nearby reefs (extra cost)
  • Diving: Scuba diving for certified divers (extra cost)
  • Dolphin Tour: Early morning dolphin watching tour (extra cost)
  • Village Visit: Visit local fishing village (often included)

Lunch: Lunch at resort or beachside restaurant.

Afternoon: More beach time or chosen activity.

Spa Treatments: Optional spa/massage treatments at resort (extra cost).

Sunset Dhow Cruise: Optional sunset cruise on traditional dhow (extra cost).

Evening: Dinner at resort, overnight.

DAY 11: BEACH DAY OR ISLAND EXCURSION

Full Day Options: Choose from various activities:

  • Complete Relaxation: Another day at the beach/pool
  • Prison Island: Day trip to Prison Island (Changuu Island) to see giant tortoises
  • Mnemba Atoll: Full day snorkeling/diving at Mnemba Atoll Marine Conservation Area
  • Jozani Forest: Visit Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park to see red colobus monkeys
  • Kitesurfing: In Paje/Jambiani (famous for kitesurfing June-October)
  • Fishing: Deep sea fishing excursion (extra cost)

Lunch: Depending on activity - at resort, picnic, or restaurant.

Afternoon: Continue chosen activity or relaxation.

Final Zanzibar Evening: Special farewell dinner at resort or beach restaurant.

Cultural Performance: Optional traditional Taarab music or dance performance.

Last Sunset: Final Zanzibar sunset over Indian Ocean.

DAY 12: DEPARTURE FROM ZANZIBAR

Final Morning: Enjoy final Zanzibar breakfast at resort.

Last Beach Time: Final swim or walk on the beach (depending on flight time).

Check-out: Check out from resort (standard time is 10-11 AM).

Transfer to Airport: Transfer to Zanzibar Airport for departure flight.

Optional Activities: If late flight, possible:

  • Day room extension (extra cost)
  • Last-minute souvenir shopping
  • Lunch in Stone Town before flight

Flight: Depart Zanzibar for home or next destination.

Extension Options:

  • Extra beach days in Zanzibar
  • Extension to Pemba Island (more remote)
  • Mafia Island for diving enthusiasts
  • Return to mainland Tanzania for more safari

Farewell: Bid farewell to Tanzania with incredible memories of wildlife and beaches.

Essential Safari & Beach Tips

What to Pack for Both Safari & Beach

Safari Clothing: Neutral colors (khaki, green, brown), lightweight layers. Beach Wear: Swimwear, cover-ups, light cotton clothing. Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes for safari, sandals for beach. Essentials: Sunscreen (high SPF), hat, sunglasses, binoculars, camera. Health: Malaria prophylaxis, insect repellent, personal medications.

Great Migration Timing

December-March: Southern Serengeti - calving season (approximately 500,000 births). April-May: Central Serengeti - start of migration north. June-July: Western Corridor - Grumeti River crossings. August-October: Northern Serengeti - dramatic Mara River crossings. November: Return migration to Southern Serengeti.

Zanzibar Considerations

Dress Code: Modest dress in Stone Town (shoulders and knees covered). Beach Wear: Acceptable at resorts and designated beach areas. Culture: Conservative Muslim island - respect local customs. Photography: Ask permission before photographing people. Shopping: Bargaining expected in markets, fixed prices in shops.

Health & Safety

Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation from remote areas. Vaccinations: Yellow fever required, hepatitis A&B, typhoid recommended. Malaria: Both mainland and Zanzibar are malaria zones. Sun Protection: High UV in both locations - strong sunscreen essential. Water Safety: Drink bottled water, avoid ice in drinks outside resorts.

Best Time for This Safari

Dry Season (Best): June-October - best wildlife viewing, cooler temperatures. Great Migration: July-October for river crossings in Northern Serengeti. Calving Season: January-March for birthing season in Southern Serengeti. Zanzibar Weather: Best beach weather June-October, hot but pleasant. Shoulder Seasons: November & March-May - possible rain, fewer tourists.
