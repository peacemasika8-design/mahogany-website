SAFARI OVERVIEW

Kidepo Valley National Park is Uganda's most remote and wild park, offering untouched savannah landscapes, dramatic mountain backdrops, and rare wildlife species not commonly seen in other parks. This safari gives you raw African wilderness, fewer crowds, and authentic cultural encounters. Experience the untamed beauty of northeastern Uganda in true luxury style.

DETAILED ITINERARY

DAY 1: TRANSFER TO KIDEPO VALLEY NATIONAL PARK

Domestic Flight Option

1.5 - 2 hours from Entebbe to Apoka Airstrip

Road Transfer Experience

Scenic drive through Karamoja region

Flight Transfer: Board a plane from Entebbe International Airport to Apoka Airstrip. On arrival, meet our English-speaking tourist driver guide from Mahogany Travel Expeditions and receive a briefing about your 5-day Kidepo Valley National Park safari. After briefing, drive to the lodge and check-in for lunch, dinner, and overnight.

Accommodation:

Apoka Safari Lodge (Luxury Lodge)

Meal Plan: Full-board (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner)

DAY 2: GAME DRIVE IN NARUS VALLEY & AFTERNOON RELAXATION

Set off for an early morning game drive in search of Lions, Cheetahs, Elephants, Giraffes, Zebras, and lots of antelopes, with packed breakfast to the Narus Valley. Have a wonderful breakfast setup in an open savannah with a scenic view of wildlife.

Narus Valley comprises grassland plains and distant mountains and is the permanent water supply to the park's mammals. The area has excellent game drive tracks to explore numerous wildlife species such as Jackson's hartebeest, Lions, Cape buffaloes, Oribis, Rothschild's giraffes, Reedbucks, Cheetahs, and Ostriches.

After your half-day game drive to Narus Valley, return to your lodge to have refreshments and lunch, then go for your evening visit to the Manyatta communities of the Karamojong tribe.

The Karamojong people are nomadic pastoralists living in uniquely constructed houses called Manyattas that are designed in African style. A visit to Karamojong communities will expose you to their culture, art, crafts, and entertainment using their traditional sound equipment, traditional songs, and dances which will give you a breathtaking feeling.

Drive back to the lodge for Dinner and overnight.

Meal Plan: Full board (Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner)

DAY 3: KIDEPO VALLEY RHINO SANCTUARY

After having your heavy breakfast, embark on a morning game drive to Kidepo Valley Rhino Sanctuary for Rhino Tracking. This adventure will give you the opportunity to watch these endangered, magnificent creatures in their natural habitat.

After the Rhino tracking, visit the famous Hot Springs which is a glorious place to take a memorial.

Drive back to the lodge for Dinner and an overnight.

Meal Plan: Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner

DAY 4: HOT SPRING - VISIT TO THE IK PEOPLE

Full-day simple hike to visit the Ik people, some of the most isolated people in Uganda. The visit to Ik people will include charity work. The tribe on Mount Morungole offers a unique look at one of Uganda's most isolated ethnic groups.

Drive back at the lodge for Dinner and Overnight.

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner

DAY 5: DRIVE BACK TO ENTEBBE INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT OR KAMPALA CITY

Have a relaxed morning breakfast and drive to Apoka Airstrip and board a flight to Entebbe International Airport for departure.

Meal Plan: Breakfast

Safari Tips & Essential Information

Best Time to Visit

Dry Seasons (Best): June-September & December-February - wildlife concentrates around water sources, easier game viewing. Green Season: March-May & October-November - lush landscapes, fewer tourists, bird watching excellent.

What to Pack

Neutral colors: Khaki, beige, brown clothing. Layers: Mornings can be cool, afternoons hot. Camera: With zoom lens and extra batteries. Binoculars: Essential for wildlife viewing. Sunscreen & hat: Strong sun at high altitude. Comfortable walking shoes.

Health & Safety

Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate required. Malaria Prophylaxis: Strongly recommended. Travel Insurance: Must include medical evacuation. Water: Drink only bottled water provided.

About Kidepo Valley National Park

Located in the remote Karamoja region, Kidepo Valley National Park is consistently ranked among the top national parks in Africa by travel experts. Its isolation ensures an exclusive wilderness experience with wildlife adapted to the semi-arid environment. The park is home to species found nowhere else in Uganda, including cheetahs, ostriches, and the beautiful Karamoja apalis.
