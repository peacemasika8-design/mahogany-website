10 DAYS EAST AFRICA HIGHLIGHTS TOUR

Multi-Country Safari Great Migration Maasai Mara Gorilla Trekking Serengeti

The Ultimate East African Adventure Across Three Countries

Embark on the safari of a lifetime with this comprehensive 10-day journey through East Africa's most iconic destinations. This expertly crafted multi-country tour combines the very best of Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania in one seamless itinerary, offering you an unparalleled African wildlife experience. Begin in Rwanda with an unforgettable mountain gorilla trekking encounter in the misty volcanoes, then fly to Kenya for the world-famous Great Migration spectacle in the Maasai Mara, and conclude in Tanzania exploring the endless plains of the Serengeti. Experience three distinct ecosystems, encounter Africa's most charismatic wildlife, and immerse yourself in diverse cultures across three remarkable countries. With seamless logistics, expert local guides in each destination, and carefully selected accommodations, this tour represents the pinnacle of East African safari experiences.

Physical Difficulty:
Moderate (Gorilla trekking is physically demanding, game drives are comfortable with some long travel days)

✈️ Multi-Country Logistics & Flights

Flight Arrangements: This tour includes regional flights between Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania. We handle all flight bookings and transfers. Typical routing: Kigali (Rwanda) to Nairobi (Kenya), Nairobi to Serengeti (Tanzania), Serengeti to Kilimanjaro/Arusha (Tanzania) for departure.

Visa Requirements: Three countries require careful visa planning:

  • Rwanda: Visa on arrival $50 for most nationalities
  • Kenya: eVisa $51 available online before travel
  • Tanzania: Visa on arrival $50 for most nationalities (USD cash required)
  • Recommended: East African Tourist Visa $100 covering all three countries - apply online before travel

Border Crossings: Flights between countries handle immigration at airports. Have all documents (passport, visas, yellow fever certificate) readily accessible in hand luggage.

Baggage Allowance: Regional flights typically allow 15kg checked baggage + 7kg hand luggage. We recommend packing light with soft-sided bags.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN KIGALI, RWANDA - CITY ORIENTATION

Arrival & Welcome: Arrive at Kigali International Airport where our Rwanda guide will meet you. Complete immigration formalities and collect luggage.

Kigali City Tour: Begin your East African adventure with exploration of Rwanda's capital.

  • Visit Kigali Genocide Memorial for essential historical context (2-3 hours)
  • Tour of modern Kigali's development and infrastructure
  • Visit to local markets for cultural immersion
  • Lunch at recommended restaurant featuring Rwandan cuisine (own account)
  • Briefing about the Rwanda portion of your safari

Hotel Check-in: Check into your Kigali hotel for overnight stay.

  • Evening at leisure to explore Kigali's restaurants (dinner own account)
  • Optional visit to cultural centers or entertainment venues
  • Preparation for tomorrow's gorilla trekking adventure

Important Preparation: Ensure you have all required documents for Rwanda portion and pack for gorilla trekking tomorrow.

Overnight: Kigali Hotel / Similar (No meals included - own arrangements)

DAY 2: KIGALI - VOLCANOES NATIONAL PARK - GORILLA TREKKING PREPARATION

Breakfast & Departure: Early breakfast at hotel. Meet your Rwanda guide and depart for Volcanoes National Park.

Scenic Drive to Volcanoes (2.5-3 hours): Journey northwest through Rwanda's beautiful "Land of a Thousand Hills."

  • Photo stops at scenic viewpoints overlooking terraced hillsides
  • Introduction to Rwanda's geography, agriculture, and conservation efforts
  • Arrive at lodge near Volcanoes National Park by late morning
  • Check-in and lunch at lodge with views of volcanic mountains

Afternoon Cultural Experience: Engage with local communities around the park.

  • Visit to Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village or similar community project
  • Traditional dance and drumming performances
  • Interaction with former poachers turned conservationists
  • Learn about community benefits from gorilla tourism in Rwanda
  • Optional craft demonstrations and purchases

Evening Gorilla Briefing: Detailed preparation for tomorrow's gorilla trek.

  • Comprehensive briefing by lodge staff about gorilla trekking procedures
  • Distribution of gorilla trekking preparation information
  • Packing advice and equipment check
  • Dinner at lodge with opportunity to ask questions
  • Early night recommended for tomorrow's early start

Overnight: Mountain View Lodge, Rwanda / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 3: GORILLA TREKKING IN VOLCANOES NATIONAL PARK - FLY TO NAIROBI, KENYA

Early Morning Gorilla Trekking: The highlight of your Rwanda experience.

  • Early breakfast at lodge with packing of picnic lunches
  • Drive to park headquarters for briefing and group allocation
  • 2-6 hour trek through volcanic forest to find gorilla family
  • One magical hour observing mountain gorillas in natural habitat
  • Receive gorilla trekking certificate after return
  • Return to lodge by early afternoon for quick refreshment

Check-out & Transfer to Kigali: After gorilla experience, prepare for Kenya.

  • Check out from lodge
  • Scenic drive back to Kigali (2.5-3 hours)
  • Arrive at Kigali Airport for evening flight to Nairobi

Flight to Nairobi, Kenya: Regional flight to begin Kenya portion.

  • Evening flight from Kigali to Nairobi (approx. 1.5 hours)
  • Meet Kenya guide upon arrival at Nairobi Jomo Kenyatta Airport
  • Transfer to Nairobi hotel
  • Dinner at hotel or nearby restaurant (own account)
  • Briefing about Kenya portion of safari

Overnight: Nairobi Hotel / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch included, Dinner own account)

DAY 4: NAIROBI - MAASAI MARA NATIONAL RESERVE

Breakfast & Departure: Early breakfast at hotel. Begin journey to Kenya's most famous wildlife destination.

Scenic Drive to Maasai Mara (5-6 hours): Travel southwest through Kenya's beautiful landscapes.

  • Drive through the Great Rift Valley with scenic viewpoint stops
  • Introduction to Kenya's wildlife and conservation
  • Lunch en route at recommended restaurant (own account or packed)
  • Arrive at Maasai Mara National Reserve by mid-afternoon

Afternoon Game Drive: First wildlife experience in the Maasai Mara.

  • Enter the reserve for initial game viewing
  • Search for lions, elephants, giraffes, and other savanna wildlife
  • Focus on river areas for hippos and crocodiles
  • Sunset viewing in the African bush
  • Return to lodge as darkness falls

Lodge Check-in & Evening: Settle into your Maasai Mara accommodation.

  • Check-in and orientation at lodge or camp
  • Dinner with anticipation of tomorrow's full day in the Mara
  • Listen to night sounds of the African savanna

Overnight: Maasai Mara Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 5: FULL DAY MAASAI MARA - GREAT MIGRATION & BIG FIVE

Full Day Mara Exploration: Comprehensive game viewing in Kenya's premier reserve.

  • Early morning game drive when predators are most active
  • Search for the Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino
  • Focus on migration areas (seasonal - July to October for river crossings)
  • Picnic lunch at scenic location within the reserve
  • Afternoon game drive in different sectors of the Mara
  • Return to lodge by evening

Great Migration Focus (Seasonal): If visiting during migration season (July-October):

  • Visit Mara River for potential crossing sightings
  • Witness massive herds of wildebeest and zebra
  • Observe predator-prey interactions
  • Photograph one of nature's greatest spectacles

Optional Activities: Additional experiences available:

  • Hot Air Balloon Safari: Sunrise balloon flight over Mara (additional cost, book in advance)
  • Maasai Village Visit: Cultural interaction with local Maasai community
  • Nature Walk: Guided walk with Maasai warriors (where permitted)

Evening: Dinner at lodge sharing safari stories. Discussion about migration patterns and wildlife behavior.

Overnight: Maasai Mara Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 6: MAASAI MARA MORNING GAME DRIVE - FLY TO SERENGETI, TANZANIA

Final Mara Game Drive: Last opportunity in Kenya's premier reserve.

  • Early morning game drive focusing on species not yet seen
  • Sunrise photography in beautiful morning light
  • Return to lodge for breakfast
  • Check-out from lodge

Transfer to Airstrip & Flight: Journey to Tanzania begins.

  • Transfer to Maasai Mara airstrip
  • Scenic flight over the Mara-Serengeti ecosystem
  • Cross border into Tanzania (immigration handled during flight)
  • Arrive at Serengeti airstrip in Tanzania
  • Meet Tanzania guide and vehicle

Enter Serengeti National Park: Begin Tanzania safari experience.

  • Game drive en route to your Serengeti accommodation
  • Introduction to Tanzania's wildlife and conservation
  • Arrive at lodge/camp in central or northern Serengeti
  • Lunch at accommodation

Afternoon Serengeti Game Drive: First experience in Tanzania's iconic park.

  • Afternoon game drive in Serengeti plains
  • Search for wildlife including cheetahs, which are more common in Serengeti than Mara
  • Sunset in the endless plains
  • Return to accommodation for dinner

Overnight: Serengeti Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 7: FULL DAY SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

Full Day Serengeti Exploration: Immerse in Tanzania's most famous park.

  • Early morning game drive for predator activity
  • Explore different habitats: kopjes, rivers, woodlands, plains
  • Search for Serengeti's famous lion prides and leopard sightings
  • Picnic lunch at scenic location within the park
  • Afternoon game drive focusing on seasonal wildlife concentrations

Serengeti Highlights: Focus on park specialties:

  • Kopjes: Granite rock formations with unique ecosystems and lion habitats
  • Migration Viewing: Depending on season, witness herds in Serengeti (typically December-July in southern/central, August-October in northern)
  • Predator Action: Serengeti offers exceptional big cat viewing year-round
  • Birdlife: Over 500 bird species including many raptors

Optional Activities: Enhance your Serengeti experience:

  • Hot Air Balloon Safari: Sunrise flight over Serengeti (additional cost, book in advance)
  • Walking Safari: Guided bush walk (where permitted, additional cost)
  • Visit to Seronera Visitor Center: Educational center about Serengeti ecosystem

Evening: Dinner at lodge sharing stories. Comparison of Kenya's Mara and Tanzania's Serengeti experiences.

Overnight: Serengeti Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 8: SERENGETI - NGORONGORO CONSERVATION AREA

Morning Serengeti Game Drive: Final exploration before moving to next destination.

  • Morning game drive in Serengeti with packed breakfast
  • Focus on specific interests or wildlife not yet seen
  • Last photographs of Serengeti's iconic landscapes
  • Begin journey southeast toward Ngorongoro

Scenic Drive to Ngorongoro: Travel through changing landscapes.

  • Game drive exit from Serengeti National Park
  • Enter Ngorongoro Conservation Area
  • Stop at Olduvai Gorge (optional, additional fee) - "Cradle of Mankind" archaeological site
  • Continue to Ngorongoro Crater rim
  • Arrive at lodge on crater rim by late afternoon

Ngorongoro Crater Rim Experience: Settle into your crater-edge accommodation.

  • Check-in at lodge with spectacular crater views
  • Lunch at lodge (if not taken earlier)
  • Afternoon at leisure to enjoy lodge facilities and views
  • Sunset viewing over Ngorongoro Crater
  • Briefing about tomorrow's crater descent

Evening: Dinner with anticipation of tomorrow's crater adventure. The cooler temperatures at altitude (2,300m).

Overnight: Ngorongoro Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 9: NGORONGORO CRATER DESCENT - ARUSHA/KILIMANJARO

Early Crater Descent: Experience the "Eighth Wonder of the World."

  • Early breakfast at lodge
  • Descend 600m into the Ngorongoro Crater (world's largest intact caldera)
  • Full morning game drive on crater floor
  • Exceptional wildlife viewing in compact area
  • High chance of seeing black rhino (one of best places in Africa)
  • Picnic lunch at scenic crater floor location

Ngorongoro Crater Highlights: Unique ecosystem features:

  • High Wildlife Density: Approximately 25,000 large animals in 260km² area
  • Big Five Potential: Lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rare black rhino
  • Lake Magadi: Alkaline lake attracting flamingos (seasonal)
  • Lerai Forest: Fever tree forest with elephants and monkeys
  • Cultural Aspect: Maasai grazing rights within conservation area

Ascend & Departure: After crater exploration, begin journey back.

  • Ascend crater rim by early afternoon
  • Return to lodge for refreshment and check-out
  • Drive to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport area (3-4 hours)
  • Arrive at hotel near airport by evening

Farewell Dinner: Final evening celebrating your East African adventure.

  • Check into airport hotel
  • Farewell dinner with guide and team
  • Reflection on three-country experiences
  • Final packing and preparation for departure

Overnight: Arusha/Kilimanjaro Hotel / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 10: DEPARTURE FROM KILIMANJARO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT

Morning Activities (Depending on Flight Time):

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Check out from hotel (luggage can be stored if needed)
  • For afternoon/evening flights: Optional Arusha city tour including:
    • Visit to local markets for final souvenir shopping
    • Cultural heritage center visit
    • Lunch at recommended restaurant (own account)
  • For morning flights: Direct transfer to airport after breakfast

Airport Transfer: Transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for departure flight.

  • Assistance with check-in procedures
  • Farewell from Tanzania guide and team
  • Completion of exit formalities
  • Departure with unforgettable memories of three-country East African adventure

End of Tour: Our services end at Kilimanjaro International Airport after check-in. Safe travels!

Meals: Breakfast included. Other meals depending on flight schedule.
