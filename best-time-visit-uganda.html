When Is the Best Time to Visit Uganda for a Safari?

June 23, 2026 8 min read Travel Tips

One of the most common questions travelers ask when planning a Uganda safari is: "When is the best time to visit Uganda?" The simple answer is that Uganda is a year-round safari destination. Thanks to its tropical climate, moderate temperatures, and diverse landscapes, the country can be visited at any time of the year. However, certain seasons are better suited for specific activities such as gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, birdwatching, or adventure experiences.

Often referred to as the "Pearl of Africa," Uganda offers incredible wildlife encounters, breathtaking scenery, vibrant cultures, and unforgettable adventures throughout the year. Whether you're planning to track mountain gorillas in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, enjoy game drives in Queen Elizabeth National Park, or cruise along the Nile River, understanding Uganda's seasons can help you choose the perfect time for your safari.

Understanding Uganda's Climate

Uganda enjoys a pleasant tropical climate that is moderated by its high altitude, extensive lakes, mountain ranges, and vast forests. Despite being located near the Equator, the country does not experience extreme heat, with average daytime temperatures generally ranging between 21°C and 28°C (70°F to 82°F) throughout the year.

Uganda experiences two main dry seasons and two rainy seasons each year. The dry seasons occur from June to August and December to February, and are generally considered the best times for wildlife viewing, gorilla trekking, chimpanzee tracking, and other outdoor activities.

The rainy seasons occur from March to May and September to November. During these periods, the country receives more rainfall, resulting in lush green landscapes, abundant vegetation, and excellent birdwatching opportunities.

Key Climate Facts

  • Average Temperature: 21°C – 28°C (70°F – 82°F)
  • Dry Seasons: June – August & December – February
  • Rainy Seasons: March – May & September – November
  • Year-Round Destination: Activities available every month

Uganda's Tourist Seasons

High Season in Uganda

Uganda's high tourism season occurs during the dry months of June to September and from December to February. These periods are widely regarded as the best times to visit because they offer favorable weather conditions for most outdoor activities.

During the dry season, national parks are more accessible, roads are generally in better condition, and forest trails are easier to navigate. Wildlife viewing is particularly rewarding because animals often gather around rivers, lakes, and water sources, making them easier to spot.

Advantages

  • Excellent wildlife viewing
  • Dry hiking and trekking trails
  • Ideal weather for outdoor activities
  • Best photography conditions

Things to Consider

  • Higher accommodation rates
  • Limited permit availability
  • Increased visitor numbers
  • Book permits months in advance

Low Season in Uganda

The low season generally occurs during the rainy months of March to May and November. While these periods receive more rainfall, they offer unique advantages that appeal to many travelers.

Advantages

  • Lower accommodation costs
  • Fewer tourists
  • Beautiful green landscapes
  • Excellent birdwatching opportunities

Things to Consider

  • Muddy roads and forest trails
  • Some trekking routes more challenging
  • Heavy rainfall can affect travel schedules

Best Time for Wildlife Safaris

Uganda is home to some of East Africa's most impressive wildlife destinations, and the dry seasons from June to August and December to February are generally considered the best times for traditional safaris.

During these months, vegetation becomes thinner, improving visibility during game drives. Water sources become more important for wildlife, causing animals to congregate around rivers, lakes, and watering points where they are easier to observe.

Uganda's major safari destinations include Queen Elizabeth National Park, Murchison Falls National Park, Kidepo Valley National Park, and Lake Mburo National Park. Visitors can expect to see elephants, buffaloes, lions, giraffes, antelopes, hippos, crocodiles, and a wide variety of bird species.

Wildlife Viewing During the Rainy Season

Although many travelers prefer the dry season, the rainy months provide a different but equally rewarding safari experience. The parks become lush and green, creating spectacular scenery and vibrant photographic opportunities. The rainy season is also a period when many animals give birth, offering chances to observe young wildlife and increased predator activity.

Best Time for Gorilla Trekking

Mountain gorilla trekking is Uganda's most famous wildlife activity and can be undertaken throughout the year. However, the dry seasons are generally preferred due to more favorable trekking conditions.

June to August and December to February

These months offer the best trekking conditions, with drier forest trails and less challenging hiking routes. The clearer weather also improves visibility for photography and enhances the overall trekking experience.

Uganda's mountain gorillas are found in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park and Mgahinga Gorilla National Park, both of which provide unforgettable encounters with these endangered primates. Because permits are limited and demand is high, travelers planning to visit during peak season should secure their permits several months in advance.

Gorilla Trekking During the Rainy Season

The rainy season offers a unique perspective on gorilla trekking. The forests are lush, vibrant, and particularly beautiful during this time. While trails may be muddy and more physically demanding, permits are often easier to obtain and accommodations may be more affordable.

Best Time for Chimpanzee Trekking

Chimpanzee trekking is available year-round in destinations such as Kibale National Park, Budongo Forest, and Kyambura Gorge.

During the dry seasons, hiking trails are easier to navigate, making trekking more comfortable. In the rainy months, the forests become exceptionally green and productive, with abundant fruit attracting chimpanzees and other wildlife.

Best Time for Birdwatching

Uganda is considered one of Africa's premier birding destinations, with more than 1,090 recorded bird species. The country's remarkable diversity of habitats supports an incredible variety of resident and migratory birds.

The best birdwatching period generally occurs between November and April, when migratory species from Europe and North Africa are present.

Top birding destinations include Mabamba Swamp, Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, Queen Elizabeth National Park, Murchison Falls National Park, and Semuliki National Park.

Festivals and Cultural Events

Bayimba International Festival

September — East African music, dance, theater, film, and visual arts.

Festival on the Nile

August — Storytelling, traditional performances, food, music, and community activities in Jinja.

Rolex Festival

August — Celebrating Uganda's famous street food with tastings, cooking competitions, and entertainment.

Nyege Nyege Festival

One of Africa's largest music festivals celebrating African creativity, contemporary music, and cultural exchange.

Month-by-Month Overview

January – February

Dry weather, excellent wildlife viewing, ideal gorilla trekking

March – May

Rainy season, lush landscapes, fewer tourists, lower costs

June – August

Peak safari season, best wildlife viewing, ideal trekking

September – November

Short rainy season, excellent birdwatching, newborn wildlife

December

Transition to dry season, favorable safari conditions, festive celebrations

So, When Is the Best Time to Visit Uganda?

Quick Recommendation

  • For gorilla trekking: June–August and December–February
  • For wildlife safaris: June–August and December–February
  • For birdwatching: November–April
  • For budget travel: March–May and September–November
  • For photography & green landscapes: Rainy seasons
  • For festivals & cultural experiences: August–September

Conclusion

Uganda is a destination that offers remarkable experiences throughout the year. The dry seasons provide outstanding conditions for gorilla trekking, wildlife viewing, and outdoor adventures, while the rainy seasons reward travelers with lush scenery, excellent birdwatching, fewer crowds, and attractive travel discounts.

Whether you are dreaming of meeting mountain gorillas, exploring vast savannahs, cruising along the Nile, or immersing yourself in Uganda's vibrant culture, every season presents unique opportunities. The best time to visit Uganda is ultimately the time that aligns with your interests, budget, and travel goals. No matter when you choose to visit, the Pearl of Africa promises unforgettable wildlife encounters, spectacular landscapes, and warm hospitality.
