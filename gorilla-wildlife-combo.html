6 DAYS GORILLA AND WILDLIFE SAFARI

Wildlife Safari Gorilla Trekking Adventure Big Five Photography

Rwanda's Ultimate Wildlife Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 6-day journey through Rwanda's most spectacular natural wonders, combining two of Africa's premier wildlife experiences. This comprehensive safari takes you from the misty volcanic slopes of Volcanoes National Park - home to the endangered mountain gorillas - to the vast savannas of Akagera National Park, Rwanda's answer to the classic East African safari. Experience close encounters with gorilla families, track golden monkeys, enjoy game drives among lions, elephants, and rhinos, and witness Rwanda's remarkable biodiversity across dramatically different ecosystems. This perfectly balanced itinerary offers both adventure and comfort, with expert guides, quality accommodations, and seamless logistics ensuring you make the most of every moment in "The Land of a Thousand Hills."

Physical Difficulty:
Moderate (Gorilla trekking involves hiking in mountainous terrain, while game drives are comfortable)

⚠️ Important Safari Preparation

Gorilla Trekking Requirements: Gorilla permits ($1,500 per person) must be secured well in advance (minimum 6-9 months for peak seasons). Age restriction: minimum 15 years. Physical fitness required for hiking at high altitude. Pack appropriate gear: waterproof hiking boots, rain jacket, gloves, long pants, and gardening gloves for protection against stinging nettles.

Health Considerations: While Rwanda is malaria-free in high altitude areas (including Volcanoes), Akagera is in a malaria zone. Consult your doctor about prophylaxis. Yellow fever vaccination certificate required for entry. COVID-19 vaccination/testing may be required - check current regulations.

Photography: Bring quality camera equipment with zoom lenses (200-400mm ideal for wildlife). Flash photography prohibited with gorillas. Extra batteries and memory cards essential.

Detailed 6-Day Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN KIGALI - TRANSFER TO VOLCANES NATIONAL PARK

Arrival & Welcome: Arrive at Kigali International Airport where our representative will meet you. Complete immigration formalities and collect luggage.

Kigali City Tour (Optional): Depending on arrival time, enjoy a brief orientation tour of Kigali including:

  • Visit to local markets for cultural immersion
  • Short city drive to see modern Kigali's development
  • Lunch at recommended restaurant (own account)
  • Optional Kigali Genocide Memorial visit if time allows (additional time required)

Scenic Drive to Volcanoes (2.5-3 hours): Depart Kigali for the dramatic drive northwest to Volcanoes National Park.

  • Pass through Rwanda's beautiful "Land of a Thousand Hills"
  • Photo stops at scenic viewpoints overlooking terraced hillsides
  • Brief stops in local communities along the route
  • Introduction to Rwanda's geography and agriculture

Arrival at Lodge: Arrive at your accommodation near Volcanoes National Park.

  • Check-in and lodge orientation
  • Relax and acclimatize to the altitude (2,500-3,000m)
  • Evening briefing by your guide about gorilla trekking procedures
  • Distribution of gorilla trekking preparation information

Evening: Dinner at the lodge with opportunity to ask questions about the upcoming gorilla trek. Early night recommended in preparation for tomorrow's adventure.

Overnight: Mountain View Lodge / Similar (Dinner included)

DAY 2: GORILLA TREKKING IN VOLCANES NATIONAL PARK

Early Morning Preparation: Wake up early for this unforgettable day.

  • Full breakfast at the lodge with packing of picnic lunches
  • Final packing check: waterproof clothing, hiking boots, cameras, etc.
  • Drive to park headquarters (approx. 30 minutes)

Park Headquarters Briefing (7:00 AM): Arrive at Volcanoes National Park headquarters for essential pre-trek formalities.

  • Registration and gorilla group allocation based on fitness levels
  • Park ranger briefing on gorilla behavior and trekking rules
  • Introduction to trackers and guides for your assigned group
  • Cultural performance by local community groups (optional)
  • Final opportunity to rent walking sticks or hire porters ($15-20)

Gorilla Trekking (Approx. 2-6 hours): Begin your trek into the forest in search of mountain gorillas.

  • Trek duration varies (1-4 hours each way) depending on gorilla location
  • Hiking through beautiful montane forest with stunning scenery
  • Physical challenge: altitude, steep slopes, muddy trails, stinging nettles
  • Porter assistance available to carry bags and provide physical support
  • Trackers communicate with guides about gorilla movements

Gorilla Encounter (1 Hour): The magical moment of encountering a gorilla family.

  • Spend one hour observing gorillas in their natural habitat
  • Observe social interactions, feeding, playing, nesting behaviors
  • Photography opportunities (no flash, maintain 7m distance)
  • Guides provide interpretation of gorilla behavior and group dynamics
  • Experience of a lifetime - often emotional and transformative

Trek Back & Return: Hike back to the starting point.

  • Return trek typically faster than approach
  • Receive gorilla trekking certificates at park headquarters
  • Opportunity to purchase souvenirs supporting local communities
  • Return to lodge for well-deserved relaxation

Afternoon Options: Depending on energy levels and time:

  • Rest and relaxation at the lodge
  • Optional visit to Iby'Iwacu Cultural Village (additional cost)
  • Nature walk around lodge grounds
  • Massage or spa treatment at lodge (if available, additional cost)

Evening: Share gorilla encounter stories over dinner. Celebration of successful trek.

Overnight: Mountain View Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

DAY 3: GOLDEN MONKEY TRACKING OR DIAN FOSSEY GRAVE - TRANSFER TO KIGALI

Morning Option 1: Golden Monkey Tracking: For those with energy for more primate encounters.

  • Early breakfast and transfer to park headquarters
  • Briefing and allocation to golden monkey tracking groups
  • Shorter, easier trek compared to gorilla trekking (1-3 hours total)
  • One hour with playful golden monkeys in bamboo forest
  • Excellent photographic opportunities with these colorful primates
  • Return to lodge by late morning

Morning Option 2: Dian Fossey Grave Hike: For those interested in conservation history.

  • More strenuous hike to Karisoke Research Center site
  • Visit grave of Dian Fossey and her beloved gorillas
  • Learn about gorilla conservation history and challenges
  • Stunning views of volcanic peaks (if weather permits)
  • Physically demanding (4-6 hours hiking at high altitude)

Morning Option 3: Relaxation & Cultural Activities: For those preferring a gentler morning.

  • Leisurely breakfast with mountain views
  • Visit to local community projects or craft centers
  • Nature walk in lodge surroundings for bird watching
  • Relaxation with lodge facilities

Check-out & Lunch: Check out from lodge after selected morning activity. Enjoy lunch at the lodge or en route.

Return to Kigali (2.5-3 hours): Scenic drive back to Kigali with stops as desired.

  • Optional shopping for handicrafts along the route
  • Photo stops at scenic viewpoints
  • Arrival in Kigali by late afternoon

Kigali Accommodation: Check into Kigali hotel for overnight stay.

  • Evening at leisure - explore Kigali's restaurants (dinner own account)
  • Optional: Visit local markets or cultural centers
  • Rest and preparation for next phase of safari

Overnight: Kigali Hotel / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch included)

DAY 4: TRANSFER TO AKAGERA NATIONAL PARK - AFTERNOON GAME DRIVE

Breakfast & Departure: Early breakfast at hotel. Meet your new guide specialized in Akagera wildlife.

Scenic Drive to Akagera (2.5 hours): Journey east to Rwanda's only savanna park.

  • Drive through changing landscapes from hills to plains
  • Introduction to Akagera's history and conservation success story
  • Brief stops en route for refreshments and photography
  • Arrive at park entrance by late morning

Park Entry & Lodge Check-in: Complete park formalities and proceed to your accommodation.

  • Check-in and lunch at lodge with views over park
  • Settle into your room and prepare for afternoon activities
  • Briefing by lodge staff about facilities and safety procedures

Afternoon Game Drive (3:00 PM - 6:30 PM): First wildlife experience in Akagera.

  • Depart for game drive in open 4x4 safari vehicle
  • Focus on northern circuit wildlife areas
  • Search for elephants, buffalo, giraffes, zebras, antelopes
  • Bird watching opportunities - Akagera has 500+ species
  • Sunset viewing at scenic viewpoint
  • Return to lodge as darkness falls

Evening at Lodge: Dinner at lodge with opportunity to discuss sightings with guide and other guests. Night sounds of the African bush.

Overnight: Akagera Game Lodge / Similar (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included)

  • Rest before afternoon activities

Lunch & Preparation: Lunch at lodge followed by preparation for boat safari.

  • Excellent bird watching: fish eagles, kingfishers, cormorants, herons

  • Views of islands and shoreline habitats

Late Afternoon Game Drive (5:00 PM - 7:00 PM): Final game viewing opportunity.

Farewell Dinner: Special dinner at lodge celebrating your Rwandan wildlife adventure. Share stories and photos with fellow travelers.

About the National Parks

Volcanoes National Park: Located in northwestern Rwanda, this park protects the Rwandan portion of the Virunga Mountains, home to approximately one-third of the world's remaining mountain gorillas. The park covers 160 km² of montane rainforest and bamboo forest, with altitudes ranging from 2,400 to 4,507 meters. In addition to gorillas, it hosts golden monkeys, forest elephants, buffalo, and numerous bird species. The park was the base for Dian Fossey's pioneering gorilla research.

Akagera National Park: Rwanda's only savanna park, covering 1,200 km² in eastern Rwanda along the Tanzanian border. The park is named after the Kagera River which flows along its eastern boundary. After suffering significant wildlife losses during and after the 1994 genocide, Akagera has undergone remarkable restoration through partnership between the Rwanda Development Board and African Parks. Since 2010, lion and rhino have been reintroduced, completing the "Big Five." The park now hosts lions, leopards, elephants, buffalo, rhinos, giraffes, zebras, antelopes, hippos, crocodiles, and over 500 bird species.

Conservation Success: Both parks represent conservation success stories. Mountain gorilla numbers have increased from around 250 in the 1980s to over 1,000 today. Akagera has seen wildlife populations rebound dramatically with effective anti-poaching measures and community engagement programs.

Wildlife You May Encounter

Volcanoes National Park:

  • Mountain Gorillas: Approximately 10 habituated family groups available for tracking
  • Golden Monkeys: Endangered primates unique to this region
  • Forest Elephants: Smaller, elusive forest-dwelling elephants
  • Buffalo: Forest buffalo subspecies
  • Birdlife: Over 200 species including many Albertine Rift endemics
  • Other mammals: Spotted hyena, duikers, bushbuck, serval cat

Akagera National Park:

  • Big Five: Lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and both black and white rhino
  • Large Herbivores: Giraffe, zebra, hippo, eland, topi, waterbuck, impala
  • Predators: Spotted hyena, side-striped jackal, serval, genet
  • Primates: Olive baboon, vervet monkey
  • Birdlife: 500+ species including fish eagle, shoebill (rare), papyrus gonolek
  • Reptiles: Nile crocodile, various snakes and lizards

Best Viewing Times: Early morning and late afternoon for predators. Gorilla viewing depends on trekking schedule. Boat safari ideal for hippos and water birds.

Practical Information for Your Safari

Best Time to Visit: Rwanda can be visited year-round, but optimal conditions are during dry seasons:

  • June to September: Long dry season - best for gorilla trekking (less muddy) and wildlife viewing (animals congregate around water)
  • December to February: Short dry season - also excellent conditions
  • March to May & October to November: Rainy seasons - landscapes greener, fewer tourists, but trekking can be more challenging

What to Pack:

  • Gorilla Trekking: Waterproof hiking boots, rain jacket, gardening gloves, long pants, long-sleeved shirts
  • Safari Clothing: Neutral colors, light layers, hat, sunglasses
  • Equipment: Binoculars, camera with zoom lens, spare batteries, memory cards
  • Health: Personal medication, insect repellent, sunscreen, hand sanitizer
  • Documents: Passport, visa, gorilla permit copy, insurance documents, vaccination certificates

Transportation: Comfortable 4x4 safari vehicles with pop-up roofs for game viewing. Professional drivers/guides with extensive knowledge of parks and wildlife.

Physical Requirements: Gorilla trekking requires moderate fitness. Treks can involve 2-6 hours of hiking at altitudes of 2,500-3,000m over steep, sometimes muddy terrain. Porters available for hire to assist with carrying bags.

Essential Safari Tips

  • Gorilla Permits: Book minimum 6-9 months in advance for peak season. Permits are non-refundable.
  • Fitness Preparation: Moderate fitness required. Consider pre-trip walking/hiking to prepare.
  • Altitude Acclimatization: Spend first night at altitude before gorilla trekking.
  • Porter Hire: Recommended ($15-20) - supports local community and makes trekking easier.
  • Gorilla Rules: Maintain 7m distance, no flash photography, no eating/drinking near gorillas, follow guide instructions.
  • Health Precautions: Malaria prophylaxis for Akagera, yellow fever vaccination required.
  • Packing Strategy: Waterproof everything for gorilla trekking - plastic bags for electronics essential.
  • Cash: Bring USD for tips, souvenirs, and additional activities. Small bills recommended.
  • Communications: Mobile network available in most areas. Wi-Fi at lodges may be limited.
  • Flexibility: Wildlife viewing unpredictable - patience and flexibility enhance experience.
