East Africa's Premier Safari Destinations

Kenya - The Birthplace of Safari

Kenya offers classic safari experiences in the Masai Mara, Amboseli (with Kilimanjaro views), Tsavo, Samburu, and Lake Nakuru. The annual Great Migration sees over 1.5 million wildebeest and zebras cross from Tanzania's Serengeti to Kenya's Masai Mara between July and October. Kenya also boasts beautiful Indian Ocean beaches and rich Maasai culture.

Tanzania - The Safari Heartland

Tanzania is home to the Serengeti (site of the Great Migration), Ngorongoro Crater (the world's largest intact caldera), Tarangire (famous for elephants), and Selous (Africa's largest game reserve). Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak, and the spice islands of Zanzibar add to Tanzania's diverse attractions.

Uganda - The Pearl of Africa

Uganda offers exceptional primate experiences including mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and chimpanzee tracking in Kibale Forest. The mighty Nile River at Murchison Falls, tree-climbing lions in Queen Elizabeth National Park, and the snow-capped Rwenzori Mountains make Uganda uniquely diverse.

Rwanda - Land of a Thousand Hills

Rwanda provides accessible mountain gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park, chimpanzee encounters in Nyungwe Forest, and Big Five safaris in Akagera National Park. Its clean, organized cities and remarkable recovery from the 1994 genocide make Rwanda an inspiring destination.

Democratic Republic of Congo - The Adventure Frontier

DRC offers adventurous experiences including gorilla trekking in Virunga National Park (Africa's oldest national park), hiking active volcanoes like Nyiragongo with its lava lake, and tracking lowland gorillas in Kahuzi-Biega. DRC provides raw, authentic African experiences for adventurous travelers.

Key Wildlife Experiences

  • The Great Migration: Witness millions of wildebeest and zebras crossing between Serengeti and Masai Mara (July-October)
  • Mountain Gorilla Trekking: Encounter endangered gorillas in Uganda, Rwanda, or DRC (year-round)
  • Big Five Viewing: See lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino in multiple parks
  • Primate Diversity: Track chimpanzees, golden monkeys, colobus monkeys, and more
  • Bird Watching: East Africa boasts over 1,400 bird species, including many endemics

Travel Practicalities

  • Visa Requirements: East Africa Tourist Visa available for Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda. Tanzania and DRC require separate visas.
  • Best Time to Visit: Dry seasons (June-October and December-February) for wildlife viewing
  • Health Requirements: Yellow fever vaccination mandatory. Malaria prophylaxis recommended.
  • Currency: USD widely accepted. Local currencies: Kenyan Shilling, Tanzanian Shilling, Ugandan Shilling, Rwandan Franc, Congolese Franc.
  • Transport: Road transfers between parks, domestic flights available for longer distances.

Sample Itinerary: 14-Day East Africa Highlights

Days 1-3: Arrive Nairobi, Kenya. Masai Mara game drives, witness the Great Migration.
Days 4-5: Fly to Kilimanjaro, Tanzania. Tarangire National Park elephant viewing.
Days 6-7: Ngorongoro Crater descent, Serengeti game drives.
Day 8: Fly to Kigali, Rwanda. City tour including Genocide Memorial.
Days 9-10: Gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda.
Day 11: Cross to Uganda. Lake Bunyonyi relaxation.
Days 12-13: Bwindi Impenetrable Forest gorilla trekking, Uganda.
Day 14: Return to Kigali for international departure.

Conservation and Responsible Tourism

East Africa is a global leader in wildlife conservation and community-based tourism. Revenue from safari tourism directly funds:

✓ Anti-Poaching Efforts

Park fees support ranger patrols and wildlife protection

✓ Community Development

Revenue sharing supports local schools, clinics, and infrastructure

✓ Habitat Protection

Tourism creates economic value for preserving natural habitats
