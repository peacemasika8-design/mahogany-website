Detailed Luxury Itinerary

DAY 1: ARRIVAL IN ENTEBBE – VIP WELCOME

Upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by a Mahogany Travel Expeditions representative and have luxury transfer to your hotel for Check-in at a 5-star lakefront hotel overlooking Lake Victoria.

  • Sunset relaxation by the pool
  • Welcome dinner & safari briefing
Overnight: Protea Hotel

Meals: Dinner

DAY 2: FLY TO MURCHISON FALLS NATIONAL PARK

Have a Morning breakfast and board on domestic flight to Pakuba Airstrip with Scenic aerial views of the Nile River and open Savannah.

On landing, you will be received by our professional tourist guide and Transfer to Nile safari lodge for Check-in and overnight.

Have a relaxed lunch and enjoy the beautiful Wildlife around the lodge and the swimming pool.

After an Afternoon leisure, embark on an evening game drive spotting elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, and lions and have Sundowner experience in the bush.

Overnight: Nile Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 3: GAME DRIVE & PRIVATE NILE CRUISE

Sett-off with our parked breakfast Early in morning for a sunrise game drive for predators, such as Lions, Leopards, and Hyenas, and other wildlife and then have a beautiful sunrise bush breakfast.

Return back at the lodge for Lunch and refreshments.

After lunch set-off for a private boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls, with beautiful scenic moments with views of hippos, crocodiles, elephants along the riverbanks and have a guided nature walk to the top of the falls.

Drive back at the lodge for Candle lite dinner under the stars and over night.

Overnight: Nile Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 4: SCENIC TRANSFER TO KIBALE NATIONAL PARK

Have breakfast and board a flight to Fort Portal with a beautiful aerial view of the crater lakes region, Tea plantation.

On arrival you will be received by the company representative and transfer you to Check-in into an exclusive forest lodge.

Overnight: Chimpudu Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 5: CHIMPANZEE TRACKING EXPERIENCE

Have an early morning breakfast and set-off a briefing by expert rangers as we prepare to dive into the Chimpanzee tracking. One hour Observe chimps feeding, grooming, and playing directly.

After the tracking, Drive back at the lodge for lunch.

Embark on an Afternoon Bigodi Wetland community nature walk for cultural exchange.

Evening relaxation and optional spa treatment.

Overnight: Chimpudu Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 6: TRANSFER TO QUEEN ELIZABETH NATIONAL PARK

Have breakfast and board on a flight to Queen Elizabeth National park with a Scenic aerial View of the rolling hills, of mountain Rwenzori and the open savanna of queen Elizabeth national park wildlife.

Arrive at Mweya Peninsular Airstrip and transfer to mweya safari lodge for check-in and over night.

Check-in at premium lodge with Savannah views.

Evening sundowner overlooking Lake Edward.

Overnight: Mweya Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 7: SUNRISE GAME DRIVE & KAZINGA CHANNEL BOAT CRUISE

Have an Early morning breakfast and sett-off for lion tracking experience (Lion Experiential) in search for wildlife like lions, leopards, elephants, hyenas an antelopes in the Kasenyi plains the northern part of the park.

Drive back to the lodge for an early lunch and Midday relaxation.

After a midday relaxation embark on an Afternoon private boat cruise on Kazinga Channel with an scenic view, Large hippo pods, Buffalo herds and Rich bird-life.

Overnight: Mweya Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 8: ISHASHA SECTOR – TREE-CLIMBING LIONS

Have an early morning breakfast and Drive to the southern sector of the park Ishasha sector in Search for the rare tree-climbing lions, and topis.

Have a Picnic lunch in the wilderness.

After a tracking, transfer to the lodge for Evening relaxation and check-ins.

Overnight: Ishasha Jungle Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 9: TRANSFER TO BWINDI IMPENETRABLE NATIONAL PARK

Scenic Flight through terraced hills to Bwindi Impenetrable national park.

Pickup and drive though the community for Cultural encounters with local communities and Arrival at luxury echo-lodge overlooking the forest canopy.

Have lunch and Afternoon Pre-trek briefing and preparation.

Overnight: Nkuringo Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 10: GORILLA TREKKING – A LIFE-CHANGING EXPERIENCE

Have an Early morning breakfast and setoff for a guided gorilla trekking adventure. Spend one magical hour with a gorilla family, Photography and unforgettable moments.

After the trekking, Return to lodge for relaxation and lunch.

Afternoon visit to the Batwa community for cultural exchanges.

Overnight: Nkuringo Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 11: MGAHINGA GORILLA NATIONAL PARK

Have an early morning breakfast and Transfer to Mgahinga national park for golden monkey tracking.

Enjoy stunning views of the Virunga volcanoes.

Evening relaxation by fireplace.

Overnight: Lake Mutanda Resort

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 12: LAKE BUNYONYI – SCENIC RETREAT

Have an early morning breakfast and drive to lake Bunyonyi. On arrival, take a boat ride to explore the islands, specifically focusing on bird watching and visiting historical sites.

And after move to the lodge for lunch and check-in.

Engage in swimming and a short, scenic walk, followed by relaxing in a hammock.

Enjoy an evening sundowner drink while watching the sunset over the terraced hill.

Overnight: Bird Nest Resort

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 13: LAKE MBURO NATIONAL PARK

After a morning breakfast, board a scheduled domestic flight, offering a spectacular aerial view of the rolling hills of southwestern Uganda, Terraced Farmlands, crater lakes and valleys and parts of the western rift valley landscape and arrive to Mbarara airstrip.

Upon arrival, meet our safari guide and transfer to lake mburo national park for check in, lunch and overnight.

After an afternoon relaxation, Embark on an evening Nature Walk with Giraffes, Zebras, Impalas, Elands (African largest antelope), warthogs and Topis Grazing around, and then return back at the lodge for Dinner and overnight.

Overnight: Mihingo Safari Lodge

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 14: MORNING GAME DRIVE – FLIGHT BACK TO ENTEBBE

Have an Early Morning for a sunrise game drive in Lake Mburo National Park, famous for wildlife species rarely seen in other Ugandan parks. Animals you may see include: Zebras (Lake Mburo has the largest population in Uganda), Impalas, Giraffes, Buffaloes, Elands (Africa's largest antelope), Warthogs and topis and more than 350 recorded bird species in the park.

Return back at the lodge for hot Breakfast, check-out and Transfer to Mbarara Airstrip and board a flight to entebbe.

Upon arrival, you will be picked by our representative and transferred to the Hotel for accommodation.

Afternoon relaxation with lake view of lake Victoria.

Accommodation: Serena Chigo Hotel

Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

DAY 15: DEPARTURE BACK HOME

Have a Leisure breakfast and have an airport transfer to Entebbe international Airport for Departure.

End of your exclusive Mahogany Travel Expeditions safari

Meals: Breakfast

Domestic Flights Included: This luxury safari includes scheduled domestic flights between parks, offering spectacular aerial views and maximizing your time for wildlife experiences.
