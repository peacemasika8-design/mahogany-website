Honeymoon Planning & Tips

Best Time for Honeymoon Safari

Peak Wildlife (Best): June-October for dry season wildlife viewing and migration. Green Season (Lush): November-May for beautiful landscapes, fewer tourists, romantic atmosphere. Migration Timing: River crossings typically July-October in Masai Mara/Serengeti. Romantic Periods: Christmas/New Year and June-August are premium periods - book 9-12 months in advance. Shoulder Season: January-February & November-December offer good conditions with fewer crowds.

Packing for Luxury Honeymoon

Safari Chic: Neutral colors for game drives, smart casual for evenings at luxury lodges. Romantic Wear: Special outfits for celebration dinners and romantic moments. Layered Clothing: Mornings and evenings can be cool, days warm - layers work best. Premium Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes for safari activities, smart shoes for evenings. Accessories: High-quality sunglasses, hat, binoculars, camera equipment for capturing memories.

Health & Safety

Vaccinations: Yellow Fever certificate required, malaria prophylaxis recommended. Travel Insurance: Comprehensive premium insurance including medical evacuation. Medical Services: Premium lodges have medical staff, major cities have excellent hospitals. Safety: East Africa is generally safe for tourists, especially in luxury tourism circuits with private guides.

Photography & Memories

Camera Equipment: Professional-grade DSLR/mirrorless with telephoto lens (100-400mm or longer) for wildlife. Memory Cards: Bring plenty - you'll take hundreds of photos. Backup: Daily backup of photos to cloud or external drive. Professional Photos: Consider booking a professional photography session to capture your special moments.

Tipping: Budget for tips for guides, drivers, lodge staff - your concierge can provide guidance.
