Why Lake Mburo & Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary?

Unique Wildlife Combinations

This safari combines two unique wildlife experiences: Lake Mburo is the only national park in Uganda with zebras and offers walking safaris, while Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary provides the only opportunity to see rhinos in Uganda. Together they offer a comprehensive wildlife experience in a short timeframe.

Conservation Success Story

Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary represents one of Uganda's greatest conservation success stories. Established to reintroduce rhinos to Uganda after they were wiped out by poaching, the sanctuary now has a growing population of southern white rhinos that will eventually be reintroduced to national parks.

Family Friendly Safari

This safari is perfect for families with children. The activities are suitable for all ages, the travel distances are manageable, and the experiences are educational as well as exciting. The walking safari at Lake Mburo and rhino tracking at Ziwa are particularly engaging for children.

Convenient Location

Both destinations are easily accessible from Kampala and Entebbe, making this safari perfect for travelers with limited time. Lake Mburo is just 3-4 hours from Kampala, and Ziwa is conveniently located on the main route to Murchison Falls, allowing for easy combination with other safaris.

Lake Mburo Wildlife Highlights:

  • Zebras: Large herds of Burchell's zebras (only park in Uganda with zebras)
  • Impalas: Graceful antelopes in large numbers
  • Topi: Distinctive antelope with dark "stockings"
  • Eland: World's largest antelope (can weigh up to 1,000 kg)
  • Warthogs: Common throughout the park
  • Buffalo: Smaller herds than in larger parks
  • Hippos: Large pods in Lake Mburo
  • Crocodiles: Nile crocodiles along lake shores
  • Leopards: Present but rarely seen
  • Hyenas: Both spotted and striped varieties
Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary Information

Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary is a 70 square kilometer private wildlife sanctuary established in 2005 to reintroduce rhinos to Uganda. The sanctuary is home to southern white rhinos that are monitored 24/7 by rangers. Visitors can track rhinos on foot with experienced guides. The sanctuary also protects other wildlife including antelopes, monkeys, and over 300 bird species. All fees contribute directly to rhino conservation and protection.

Essential Safari Tips

What to Pack for This Safari

Clothing: Neutral colors, comfortable walking shoes, hat, sunglasses, light jacket for evenings. Rhino Tracking: Closed shoes (no sandals), long pants for protection from vegetation. Essentials: Binoculars, camera with zoom lens, sunscreen, insect repellent. Walking Safari: Small daypack, water bottle, camera with strap. Documents: Passport, copy of travel insurance.

Photography Tips

Rhino Photography: Use 70-200mm or 100-400mm lens, maintain distance, follow ranger instructions. Walking Safari: Camera with strap for hands-free walking, fast shutter for moving animals. Boat Safari: Polarizing filter reduces water glare, faster shutter for moving boat. Light: Early morning and late afternoon provide best lighting. Ethics: No flash photography near rhinos, respect animal space.

Health & Safety

Rhino Tracking Safety: Always follow ranger instructions, maintain safe distance, move quietly. Walking Safari Safety: Stay with group, follow guide, don't approach animals too closely. Malaria Prevention: Prophylaxis recommended, use insect repellent, cover up at dusk. Sun Protection: High SPF sunscreen, hat, sunglasses. Hydration: Drink plenty of water, especially during walking activities.

Making the Most of Your Safari

Early Starts: Wildlife is most active early morning. Guide Knowledge: Ask questions - guides have extensive knowledge of wildlife and conservation. Children: Excellent safari for children - educational and exciting. Conservation Mindset: Understand that your fees support wildlife conservation. Flexibility: Be prepared for itinerary adjustments based on wildlife movements.

Best Time for This Safari:

  • Dry Seasons (Best): June-September & December-February - better wildlife viewing
  • Green Seasons: March-May & October-November - lush scenery, fewer tourists
  • Rhino Tracking: Year-round - rhinos are always present at Ziwa
  • Bird Watching: November-April for migratory birds
  • Walking Safaris: Dry seasons when trails are less muddy
  • Family Travel: School holidays (April, August, December) - book early
