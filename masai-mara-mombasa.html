Safari and Beach - The Perfect Kenyan Combination

Experience the ultimate Kenyan holiday with this 10-day safari and beach combination. Begin with 4 days of thrilling wildlife viewing in the world-famous Masai Mara National Reserve, home to the Big Five and the spectacular Great Migration. Then fly to the tropical coast for 5 days of relaxation on Mombasa's pristine beaches, with optional water sports, cultural tours, and historical explorations. This itinerary offers the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation.

Activity Level:
Easy to Moderate (Mostly game drives and beach relaxation)
2.5 Million
Wildebeest in the Great Migration (July-October)

Safari Highlights:

  • Masai Mara Game Drives: Daily morning and afternoon game drives in 4x4 vehicles
  • Big Five Encounters: Opportunities to see lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino
  • Great Migration: Witness the river crossings (seasonal, typically July-October)
  • Masai Cultural Visit: Optional visit to a traditional Masai village
  • Hot Air Balloon Safari: Optional sunrise balloon flight over the Mara (extra cost)
  • Mombasa Beaches: 5 nights at a beach resort with Indian Ocean views
  • Water Sports: Optional snorkeling, diving, sailing, and more
  • Historical Tours: Explore Mombasa's rich Swahili history and culture
  • All-Inclusive Resort: Most beach resorts are all-inclusive with meals and drinks
  • Internal Flight: Flight from Nairobi to Mombasa included

Detailed Safari & Beach Itinerary

SAFARI PHASE

DAY 1: ARRIVE NAIROBI, TRANSFER TO MASAI MARA

Morning Arrival: Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, Nairobi.

Airport Meet & Greet: Welcome by your safari guide and transfer to your vehicle.

Briefing: Short briefing about your safari and journey ahead.

Drive to Masai Mara: Scenic 5-6 hour drive through the Great Rift Valley with photo stops.

Lunch en route: Stop at a restaurant with views of the Rift Valley escarpment.

Enter Masai Mara: Arrive at the reserve in the late afternoon.

Evening Game Drive: Your first game drive en route to your lodge/camp.

Lodge Check-in: Arrive at your accommodation in the heart of the reserve.

Sundowner: Enjoy a drink while watching the African sunset.

Welcome Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with briefing about next day's activities.

Night Sounds: Fall asleep to the sounds of the African wilderness.

SAFARI PHASE

DAY 2: FULL DAY MASAI MARA GAME VIEWING

Early Morning: Wake up call with tea/coffee delivered to your room.

Morning Game Drive: Depart at sunrise for the best wildlife viewing - predators are most active.

Breakfast in the Bush: Enjoy a packed breakfast at a scenic spot in the reserve.

Continue Game Viewing: Search for the Big Five and other wildlife.

Optional Balloon Safari: For those who booked this extra activity (departs very early).

Return to Lodge: Return to your accommodation for lunch and rest during the heat of the day.

Afternoon Relaxation: Time to relax, swim, or enjoy lodge facilities.

Afternoon Game Drive: Depart at 4:00 PM for evening game viewing.

Sundowner: Stop for drinks while watching another spectacular African sunset.

Night Game Drive: Return to lodge after dark with spotlight for nocturnal animals.

Dinner: Dinner at the lodge with opportunity to share wildlife sightings.

SAFARI PHASE

DAY 3: MASAI MARA FULL DAY & CULTURAL VISIT

Full Day Safari Option: Choose between full day in the reserve with packed lunch or two separate game drives.

Mara River Visit: Head to the Mara River (seasonal) to see hippos, crocodiles, and possibly migration crossings.

Picnic Lunch: Enjoy lunch at a scenic spot overlooking the plains.

Afternoon Exploration: Visit different areas of the vast reserve.

Optional Masai Village Visit: Visit a traditional Masai village to learn about their culture (extra cost).

Return to Lodge: Late afternoon return to your accommodation.

Relaxation Time: Time to freshen up and relax.

Farewell Safari Dinner: Special bush dinner or traditional meal at the lodge.

Night Photography: Opportunity for night sky photography (weather permitting).

SAFARI PHASE

DAY 4: FINAL MORNING GAME DRIVE & FLY TO MOMBASA

Final Morning Game Drive: Early morning game drive for last wildlife photography opportunities.

Return for Breakfast: Return to lodge for full breakfast.

Check-out: Pack up and check out of your safari accommodation.

Drive to Nairobi: 5-6 hour drive back to Nairobi with lunch en route.

Optional Nairobi Activities: If time permits, short visit to Nairobi attractions.

Transfer to Wilson Airport: For your scheduled flight to Mombasa.

Flight to Mombasa: Approximately 1-hour flight to the coast.

Mombasa Arrival: Met by representative and transfer to your beach resort.

Beach Resort Check-in: Arrive at your beachfront accommodation.

Evening at Leisure: Time to relax and enjoy the resort facilities.

Dinner: First dinner at your beach resort (usually all-inclusive).

BEACH PHASE

DAYS 5-9: MOMBASA BEACH RELAXATION

Beach Holiday: 5 full days to relax and enjoy Mombasa's beautiful beaches.

Typical Day Structure:

  • Morning: Breakfast at the resort, morning beach walk, swim in the ocean or pool
  • Mid-day: Sunbathing, reading, relaxing under palm trees
  • Lunch: Lunch at the resort (all-inclusive)
  • Afternoon: Water sports, excursions, or more relaxation
  • Evening: Sundowners on the beach, dinner, entertainment

Optional Activities (extra cost):

  • Snorkeling & Diving: Explore Kenya's coral reefs and marine life
  • Dhow Cruise: Traditional sailboat cruise with seafood dinner
  • Mombasa Old Town: Explore the historic Swahili architecture and markets
  • Fort Jesus: UNESCO World Heritage Site - 16th-century Portuguese fort
  • Haller Park: Wildlife sanctuary with giraffe feeding
  • Deep Sea Fishing: Fishing excursions for marlin, sailfish, tuna
  • Spas & Wellness: Massages and treatments at resort spas
  • Local Markets: Shopping for souvenirs, crafts, and spices

All-Inclusive Benefits: Most meals, drinks, and non-motorized water sports included at resort.

Evening Entertainment: Most resorts offer nightly entertainment - cultural shows, bands, etc.

BEACH PHASE

DAY 10: DEPARTURE FROM MOMBASA

Final Morning: Enjoy your last breakfast with ocean views.

Last Swim: Final swim in the ocean or pool.

Check-out: Check out of your beach resort (usually by 11:00 AM).

Day Room/Late Check-out: May be available depending on flight time.

Optional Last-Minute Activities: Depending on flight schedule:

  • Last-minute shopping for souvenirs
  • Short visit to Mombasa Old Town
  • Lunch at a beachfront restaurant

Transfer to Airport: Transfer to Moi International Airport, Mombasa.

Flight: Depart on your international flight or connect via Nairobi.

Extension Options: Consider extending with:

  • Zanzibar island hopping
  • Amboseli National Park with Kilimanjaro views
  • Tsavo East or West National Parks
  • Lamu Island - more traditional Swahili culture

Farewell: Bid farewell to Kenya with amazing safari and beach memories.

Why Choose Kenya for Safari & Beach?

World-Class Wildlife

Masai Mara is consistently ranked among the world's top wildlife destinations. It offers excellent year-round game viewing and hosts the spectacular Great Migration from July to October - one of nature's greatest wildlife spectacles.

Beautiful Beaches

Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline boasts pristine white sand beaches, turquoise waters, and vibrant coral reefs. The beach resorts offer excellent facilities, water sports, and the perfect environment for relaxation after your safari.

Easy Logistics

Kenya has excellent tourism infrastructure with daily flights between Nairobi and Mombasa, making the safari-beach combination seamless. The journey from wildlife to waves takes just over an hour by air.

Cultural Diversity

Experience both traditional Masai culture in the Mara and rich Swahili culture on the coast. This combination offers unique insights into Kenya's diverse heritage and traditions.

Masai Mara Wildlife Viewing Guide

🦁
The Big Five

Lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino - Masai Mara is one of the best places in Africa to see all five.

🐆
Predators

Excellent cheetah and leopard viewing. The Mara's open plains are perfect for spotting these elusive cats.

🦓
Great Migration

July-October: Witness millions of wildebeest and zebra crossing the Mara River - a truly spectacular sight.

🦒
Other Wildlife

Giraffe, hippo, crocodile, hyena, antelope species, and over 450 bird species including many raptors.

Masai Mara Game Viewing Tips:

  • Best Times: Early morning (6:30-10:00 AM) and late afternoon (4:00-6:30 PM) when animals are most active
  • Patience Pays: Wildlife viewing requires patience - stay at sightings for a while
  • Binoculars: Essential for distant sightings and bird watching
  • Camera Ready: Keep your camera accessible at all times - sightings can happen suddenly
  • Guide Communication: Listen to your guide - they know animal behavior and the best locations
  • Respect Wildlife: Never feed animals, stay in your vehicle during game drives
  • Seasonal Considerations: Dry season (June-October) for migration; wet season (November-May) for birding and fewer crowds
Balloon Safari Over the Mara

For a truly unforgettable experience, consider adding a hot air balloon safari (extra cost). Departing at sunrise, you'll float silently over the Mara plains as wildlife awakens below. The flight lasts about an hour and concludes with a champagne breakfast in the bush. Advance booking is essential, especially during peak season.

Mombasa Beach Experience

Mombasa offers a perfect tropical beach holiday with a fascinating cultural backdrop. The North Coast (Nyali, Bamburi, Shanzu) features luxurious resorts, while the South Coast (Diani, Tiwi) offers more secluded beaches. The Swahili culture, delicious seafood, and warm Indian Ocean waters create an idyllic post-safari relaxation destination.

Essential Tips for Your Kenya Safari & Beach Holiday

Packing for Both Environments

Safari Clothing: Neutral colors (khaki, green, brown), lightweight layers, warm jacket for cool mornings. Beach Clothing: Swimwear, cover-ups, light cotton clothing, sandals. Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes for safari, sandals/flip-flops for beach. Sun Protection: High SPF sunscreen, hat, sunglasses - essential for both safari and beach.

Health & Safety

Malaria: Both Masai Mara and Mombasa are malaria areas - prophylaxis recommended. Yellow Fever: Vaccination certificate may be required depending on travel history. Travel Insurance: Comprehensive insurance including medical evacuation. Water Safety: Drink bottled water only, even when brushing teeth on safari. Sun Safety: African sun is intense - reapply sunscreen frequently.

Photography Tips

Safari Photography: Telephoto lens (70-200mm minimum), beanbag for vehicle window, dust protection. Beach Photography: Wide-angle for landscapes, polarizing filter for blue skies/water. Memory Cards: Bring plenty - you'll take more photos than you expect. Batteries: Charge every night, bring power banks. Backup: Daily backup of photos to cloud or external drive.

Cultural Sensitivity

Masai Culture: Always ask permission before taking photos of people. Beach Culture: While beach resorts are liberal, outside resorts dress modestly. Bargaining: Expected in markets but be respectful. Tipping: Customary for good service - guides, drivers, hotel staff.

Best Time for Masai Mara & Mombasa:

  • Great Migration Season (Best): July-October - prime wildlife viewing in Mara, pleasant beach weather
  • Dry Season (Excellent): January-February & June-October - good wildlife, great beach conditions
  • Green Season (Good Value): March-May & November - lusher landscapes, fewer tourists, occasional rain
  • Christmas/NY (Peak): December - busy everywhere, book well in advance
  • Year-Round: Kenya offers good wildlife and beach experiences throughout the year
Flight Information

Internal Flight: Nairobi to Mombasa flight takes approximately 1 hour. Baggage Allowance: Usually 15kg checked + 5kg hand luggage on domestic flights. Airport Transfers: Included between airports and accommodations. Flight Times: Multiple daily flights between Nairobi and Mombasa offer flexibility.
