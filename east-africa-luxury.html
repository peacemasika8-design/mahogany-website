Premium Accommodations - East Africa's Finest

&Beyond Bateleur Camp - Masai Mara, Kenya

Set in the private Kichwa Tembo concession, this iconic camp offers just nine suites with private butlers, plunge pools, and breathtaking views of the Mara. Known for its Hemingway-style elegance and exceptional service.

Singita Sasakwa Lodge - Serengeti, Tanzania

Perched on a hill overlooking the Serengeti plains, this Edwardian-style manor house offers unparalleled luxury with private cottages, infinity pool, tennis court, and world-class cuisine. Consistently ranked among Africa's best lodges.

One&Only Gorilla's Nest - Volcanoes, Rwanda

Nestled at the foothills of the Virunga volcanoes, this luxurious retreat offers stunning mountain views, private butler service, and an exceptional spa. The perfect base for gorilla trekking with ultimate comfort.

Angama Mara - Masai Mara, Kenya

Famous for its "Out of Africa" views, this contemporary lodge features glass-fronted suites with private decks, a photography studio, and exceptional guiding. Consistently praised for its design and location.

Four Seasons Safari Lodge - Serengeti, Tanzania

The only permanent luxury lodge in the central Serengeti, offering stunning infinity pools overlooking a watering hole, spacious rooms with private plunge pools, and the impeccable service Four Seasons is renowned for.

Luxury Safari Planning & Tips

Best Time for Luxury Safari

Peak Wildlife (Best): June-October for dry season wildlife viewing and migration. Green Season (Lush): November-May for beautiful landscapes, fewer tourists, bird watching. Gorilla Trekking: Year-round in Rwanda, but dry season (June-September, December-February) offers easier trekking. Migration Timing: River crossings typically July-October in Masai Mara/Serengeti. Premium Periods: Christmas/New Year and July-August are premium periods - book well in advance.

Packing for Luxury Safari

Safari Chic: Neutral colors for game drives, smart casual for evenings at luxury lodges. Layered Clothing: Mornings and evenings can be cool, days warm - layers work best. Premium Footwear: Comfortable walking shoes for trekking, smart shoes for evenings. Accessories: High-quality sunglasses, hat, binoculars, camera equipment. Evening Wear: Most luxury lodges have smart casual dress codes for dinner.

Health & Safety

Vaccinations: Yellow Fever certificate required, malaria prophylaxis recommended. Travel Insurance: Comprehensive premium insurance including medical evacuation. Medical Services: Premium lodges have medical staff, major cities have excellent hospitals. Safety: East Africa is generally safe for tourists, especially in luxury tourism circuits.

Photography & Technology

Camera Equipment: Professional-grade DSLR/mirrorless with telephoto lens (100-400mm or longer). Accessories: Extra batteries, memory cards, dust protection, beanbag for vehicle. Charging: All luxury lodges have electricity and charging facilities. Connectivity: Wi-Fi available at all properties (may be limited in remote areas).

Budget & Value

Inclusive Nature: Most costs are included - meals, drinks, activities, transfers. Extras: Premium spirits, spa treatments beyond included ones, shopping, additional optional activities. Tipping: Budget for tips for guides, drivers, lodge staff - your concierge can provide guidance. Value: Premium pricing reflects exclusive experiences, private services, and luxury accommodations.

Booking Process for Luxury Safari:

  • Advance Planning: Book 9-12 months in advance for best availability, especially for peak seasons
  • Consultation: Personal consultation with luxury safari specialist to understand preferences
  • Custom Proposal: Detailed customized itinerary and proposal developed for your approval
  • Reservations: Provisional reservations held while finalizing details
  • Permits: Gorilla permits secured immediately upon confirmation (limited availability)
  • Pre-Travel Preparation: Comprehensive pre-travel information, packing lists, visa assistance
  • Concierge Service: Dedicated concierge available throughout planning and journey
Visa Requirements

Kenya: E-visa available online for most nationalities. Tanzania: E-visa available or visa on arrival for most nationalities. Rwanda: Visa on arrival for most nationalities, or East Africa Tourist Visa covering all three countries available. Assistance: We provide guidance and assistance with all visa applications.
