7-DAY MAASAI MARA GREAT MIGRATION SAFARI

Migration Big Cats Photography Cultural Luxury

The Greatest Wildlife Spectacle on Earth

Experience the awe-inspiring Great Migration in Kenya's Maasai Mara - one of the Seven Natural Wonders of Africa. This 7-day safari is perfectly timed to witness millions of wildebeest, zebra, and antelope crossing the Mara River in a dramatic battle for survival against waiting crocodiles and predators. This is wildlife viewing at its most spectacular and raw, offering front-row seats to nature's most incredible show.

Safari Difficulty:
Easy to Moderate (Game drives only)

Migration Calendar - Best Viewing Times

July - Early August: Herds arrive in Maasai Mara from Serengeti
August - September: Peak river crossing period at Mara River
October: Herds spread across Mara plains, excellent game viewing
November: Beginning of return journey to Serengeti

  • Hot Air Balloon Safari: Optional sunrise flight over migration herds (additional cost)
  • Night Game Drives: Experience nocturnal wildlife in private conservancies
  • Maasai Village Visit: Authentic cultural interaction and traditional dancing
  • Bush Meals: Breakfast and lunch in the bush surrounded by wildlife
  • Photography Opportunities: Prime locations for professional wildlife photography

Detailed Safari Itinerary

DAY 1: NAIROBI TO MAASAI MARA

Morning: Pick up from your Nairobi hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Briefing with your safari guide about the Great Migration and what to expect.

Scenic Drive: Journey through the Great Rift Valley with stop at viewpoints for photography. Descend into the valley with its spectacular escarpments.

Lunch: Stop in Narok town for lunch at a local restaurant.

Afternoon Arrival: Enter Maasai Mara National Reserve and enjoy your first game drive en route to your camp.

Evening: Sunset game drive focusing on predators starting their evening hunts.

Accommodation: Check into your luxury tented camp. Welcome drinks and briefing about migration movements.

Dinner: First night dinner under the African stars with sounds of the wilderness.

DAY 2-3: MARA RIVER CROSSINGS & MIGRATION TRACKING

Full Migration Days: Two full days dedicated to finding and witnessing the migration.

Early Starts: Pre-dawn wake-up with coffee/tea. Depart camp at first light when predators are most active.

River Crossing Vigil: Spend mornings at strategic Mara River crossing points waiting for herds to gather courage to cross.

Bush Breakfast: Enjoy breakfast in the bush at a scenic location overlooking migration herds.

Afternoon Tracking: Track movement of herds across the plains. Witness predator action as lions and cheetahs hunt.

Picnic Lunch: Lunch in the field to maximize wildlife viewing time.

Evening Returns: Return to camp at sunset after full days of incredible wildlife sightings.

Campfire Stories: Share experiences around the campfire with fellow safari guests.

Optional Night Drive: In private conservancies, enjoy night game drives to see nocturnal animals (additional cost).

DAY 4: MIGRATION PLAINS & PREDATOR ACTION

Full Day Exploration: Explore different sectors of Maasai Mara following migration herds.

Predator Focus: Today's focus is on big cats - lions, cheetahs, and leopards taking advantage of abundant prey.

Mara Triangle: Visit the Mara Triangle, known for excellent game viewing and fewer vehicles.

Bush Lunch: Special bush lunch at a scenic location with views of migrating herds.

Afternoon Photography: Optimal photography conditions with golden afternoon light on the plains.

Cultural Interaction: Late afternoon visit to authentic Maasai village (optional, additional cost). Learn about traditional pastoralist lifestyle.

Sundowner: Evening drinks at a scenic viewpoint overlooking the Mara plains.

DAY 5: HOT AIR BALLOON & PRIVATE CONSERVANCY

Optional Balloon Safari: Pre-dawn departure for hot air balloon flight over migration herds (additional cost, highly recommended).

Champagne Breakfast: If doing balloon safari, enjoy champagne bush breakfast upon landing.

Private Conservancy Visit: Visit one of Maasai Mara's private conservancies for exclusive game viewing away from crowds.

Walking Safari: Guided bush walk with armed ranger (in conservancies where permitted).

Conservation Learning: Visit conservancy headquarters to learn about community-based conservation efforts.

Full Day Exploration: Continue tracking migration movements and predator interactions.

Bush Dinner: Special bush dinner under the stars with traditional Maasai entertainment.

DAY 6: FINAL MIGRATION DAY & PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography Focus: Final full day dedicated to capturing the perfect migration images.

Professional Tips: Your guide will help position vehicle for optimal photography conditions.

River Crossing Return: Return to best crossing points for final opportunities to witness dramatic river crossings.

Big Five Focus: Ensure you've seen all Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino.

Farewell Game Drive: Final extensive game drive covering areas you haven't yet explored.

Special Sundowner: Final sunset drinks at a memorable location with panoramic views.

Farewell Dinner: Celebration dinner at camp with traditional roast and sharing of migration stories.

DAY 7: FINAL GAME DRIVE & RETURN TO NAIROBI

Morning Game Drive: Early morning game drive for final wildlife sightings and migration herds.

Breakfast at Camp: Final breakfast at your tented camp.

Departure: Begin journey back to Nairobi with game drive en route out of the reserve.

Lunch en route: Stop for lunch in Narok town.

Rift Valley Photos: Final photo stop at Great Rift Valley viewpoint.

Arrival in Nairobi: Drop off at your Nairobi hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for departure flight.

Optional Nairobi Extension: Add nights in Nairobi to visit David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, Giraffe Centre, or Karen Blixen Museum.

The Great Migration Experience

What is the Great Migration? The annual migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest, 200,000 zebra, and 300,000 gazelle between Tanzania's Serengeti and Kenya's Maasai Mara in search of water and fresh grazing. This continuous clockwise movement covers approximately 1,800 miles and is driven by rainfall patterns.

Mara River Crossings: The most dramatic part of the migration occurs at the Mara River, where herds must cross crocodile-infested waters. These crossings can involve thousands of animals and are a life-and-death struggle that provides unparalleled wildlife viewing opportunities.

Predator Activity: The migration attracts large numbers of predators including lions, cheetahs, leopards, and hyenas. The abundance of prey creates exceptional opportunities to witness hunting behavior and predator-prey interactions.

Why 7 Days? The extended duration allows for maximum migration viewing opportunities. Migration movements are unpredictable, and more time increases your chances of witnessing river crossings and predator action.

Safari Essentials & Photography Tips

  • Best Time to Go: July to October for river crossings. Early July for arrival, August-September for peak crossings.
  • Booking Timeline: Book 6-12 months in advance for peak season. Migration camps fill quickly.
  • What to Pack:
    • Neutral-colored clothing (khaki, green, brown)
    • Warm layers for cool mornings and evenings
    • Good quality binoculars (essential)
    • Camera with telephoto lens (300mm+ recommended)
    • Sun protection (hat, sunscreen, sunglasses)
    • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Photography Tips:
    • Bring multiple memory cards and batteries
    • Use bean bag for vehicle window stabilization
    • Shoot in early morning and late afternoon for best light
    • Be patient at river crossings - can take hours
    • Focus on action and interaction, not just animals
  • Health & Safety:
    • Yellow fever vaccination required
    • Malaria prophylaxis recommended
    • Travel insurance with medical evacuation essential
    • Stay in vehicle during game drives (except designated areas)
  • Cultural Respect:
    • Always ask permission before photographing Maasai people
    • Respect local customs and traditions
    • Support community projects through responsible tourism
